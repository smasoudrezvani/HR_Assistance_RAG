# Adoption Leave Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 supports employees who are growing their families through adoption. This policy outlines adoption leave entitlements for primary and non-primary caregivers across all regions.

## Entitlements by Region

### Netherlands (NL)
- The primary caregiver is entitled to **6 weeks of adoption leave** (paid via UWV adoption benefit)
- The non-primary caregiver is entitled to **1 week paid partner leave** at 100% of salary
- Both parents are entitled to take adoption leave within a period starting 4 weeks before the adoption and ending 22 weeks after placement
- Leave may be spread over a longer period with HR agreement

### South Africa (ZA)
- The primary caregiver adopting a child under the age of 2 is entitled to **10 consecutive weeks of unpaid leave**
- The non-primary caregiver is entitled to **10 consecutive days**
- Leave begins from the date the adoption is granted by a court

### Remote (Global)
- The primary caregiver adopting a child under the age of 6 months is entitled to **8 consecutive weeks paid** at 100% salary
- The non-primary caregiver is entitled to **1 standard working week paid** at 100% salary
- Employees adopting older children (6 months–5 years) are entitled to discuss arrangements with HR

## Who Qualifies?

This policy applies to employees adopting:
- A child through a recognized legal adoption process
- A foster child placed with the expectation of legal adoption

Both parents in same-sex couples and non-traditional family structures are fully covered.

## Notification Requirements

Employees must notify HR as soon as the adoption placement is confirmed and in any case at least:
- **4 weeks in advance** of the intended leave start date

Include the following in your notification to hr@call360.com:
- Intended leave start date
- Expected placement/adoption date
- Planned return-to-work date

## Documentation Required

Employees will be asked to provide official documentation confirming:
- Legal adoption proceedings or confirmed foster placement
- Expected start date (if known in advance)

All documents are treated with strict confidentiality.

## Returning to Work

Employees returning from adoption leave are entitled to return to the same role or an equivalent role. A phased return may be requested.
