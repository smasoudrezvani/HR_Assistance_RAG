# Resignation Process
**Call360 | Version 1.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

Leaving Call360 is a significant transition. This document explains the steps to follow when an employee chooses to resign.

## Step 1: Give Notice

Submit your resignation in writing to:
- Your direct manager
- The People & Culture team at hr@call360.com

Your resignation letter should include:
- Your full name and role
- Your intended last working day (based on your contractual notice period)
- A brief, professional statement

Verbal resignations are not sufficient — a written resignation is required to begin the formal process.

## Notice Periods

Your notice period is specified in your employment contract. Standard notice periods:

| Tenure | Notice Period |
|---|---|
| Probation | 1 week |
| Less than 1 year | 1 month |
| 1–5 years | 1 month (NL) / 4 weeks (ZA) |
| 5+ years | 2 months (NL) / per contract (ZA) |

Notice begins on the day your written resignation is formally acknowledged by HR.

## Step 2: Manager Conversation

After submitting your resignation, schedule a conversation with your manager to:
- Agree how to communicate the news to the team
- Begin planning the handover of your work

## Step 3: Handover

A complete handover is expected before your last day. This includes:
- Documenting all ongoing projects and tasks in Confluence
- Briefing your colleagues or successor
- Transferring ownership of files and email threads
- Updating project tools (Jira, etc.) with current status

Your manager will confirm when the handover is satisfactorily complete.

## Step 4: Equipment Return

Return all company equipment on or before your last working day:
- Laptop, charger, and accessories
- Building access badge
- Any other company property (headsets, monitors, etc.)

Remote employees: HR will coordinate equipment collection.

## Step 5: Exit Survey

An exit survey is sent automatically via PeopleBase in your final month. Completing it is optional but strongly encouraged — your honest feedback helps Call360 improve.

## Step 6: IT Offboarding

On your last day, IT will:
- Disable your accounts and access credentials
- Transfer ownership of your files and emails as instructed by your manager
- Coordinate the return and wiping of your device

## Final Pay

Your final paycheck will be processed in the usual payroll cycle and will include:
- Salary for days worked
- Unused vacation payout (where applicable per regional law)
- Outstanding expenses

## Staying Connected

Call360 maintains an alumni network. If you'd like to stay connected, ask HR about joining the alumni Slack channel before you leave.
