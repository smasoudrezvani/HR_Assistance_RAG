# Sick Leave Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.5 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 supports employees during illness and recognizes that taking time off when unwell protects both the individual and the broader team. This policy outlines sick leave entitlements, reporting procedures, and long-term illness support.

## Reporting Sick Leave

If you are unwell and unable to work, you must:

1. Notify your manager **before the start of your working day** via Slack or phone
2. Also notify the People & Culture team by emailing hr@call360.com
3. Update your status in PeopleBase under **Time Off → Sick Leave**

Do not wait until you return to work to report sick leave. Absence without notification may be recorded as unauthorized absence.

## Entitlements by Region

### Netherlands (NL)
- Sick leave is governed by Dutch law
- There is **no cap** on short-term sick leave
- Employees sick for more than **2 weeks** are referred to the company occupational health doctor (arboarts)
- Long-term sick leave is paid at **100% for the first 52 weeks**, then **70% for weeks 53–104**
- Re-integration obligations apply under Dutch law after extended absence

### South Africa (ZA)
- Employees are entitled to **30 days paid sick leave per calendar year** (pro-rated)
- Sick leave does not carry over to the following year and is not paid out upon termination
- A **doctor's note is required** for absences longer than 2 consecutive workdays

### Remote (Global)
- Short-term sick leave up to **2 consecutive days** requires no documentation
- For absences longer than 2 days, a **written doctor's note** is required within 5 business days
- Call360 reimburses long-term sick leave once per calendar year for a **maximum of 2 weeks**

## Doctor's Note Requirements

For NL employees: required after 5 consecutive sick days (or as requested by HR/occupational doctor).
For ZA and Remote employees: required after 2 consecutive sick days.

Failure to provide a required note may result in the absence being deducted from vacation leave or treated as unpaid leave.

## Long-Term Illness

For absences lasting more than 4 weeks, HR will proactively reach out to discuss:
- A phased return-to-work plan
- Adjustments to duties or working hours
- Access to the Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

Call360 is committed to supporting employees through periods of extended illness and will work collaboratively on return-to-work plans.

## Mental Health Days

Call360 encourages employees to take sick leave when experiencing mental health challenges. Mental health is treated with the same respect and confidentiality as physical illness. Employees are encouraged to contact the EAP for additional support.
