# Onboarding Process
**Call360 | Version 2.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Welcome to Call360!

We want every new employee to feel informed, connected, and productive as quickly as possible. This guide walks through the Call360 onboarding journey.

## Before Day 1 (Pre-Boarding)

HR will contact you before your start date to:
- Confirm your equipment preferences and delivery address (remote employees)
- Share PeopleBase login instructions and pre-reading materials
- Arrange system access (email, Slack, Confluence)
- Send your employment contract and required forms for signature

You'll receive a welcome email with your schedule for the first week, your buddy's name, and a link to our internal Confluence onboarding page.

## Day 1 Checklist

On your first day, your manager and the People & Culture team will help you with:

- [ ] Email and Microsoft 365 account access
- [ ] Slack access — added to relevant channels
- [ ] PeopleBase login — add your personal information and photo
- [ ] 1Password setup with correct vaults
- [ ] Equipment form signed and uploaded
- [ ] Building access / security setup (Amsterdam & Cape Town offices)
- [ ] Meet your onboarding buddy
- [ ] Introduction to your immediate team

## First Week

- Attend your team's regular stand-up and meetings
- Set up all role-specific tools (guided by your manager)
- Read the Employee Handbook and key policies
- Create your personal Confluence intro page
- Schedule coffee chats with each team member

## First Month

- Meet the Leadership Team (organized by HR during the first month)
- Shadow other teams to understand how Call360 operates end-to-end
- Complete your onboarding survey in PeopleBase (sent automatically at 30 days)
- Set your Q1 OKRs with your manager
- Schedule your 30-day check-in with your manager

## 90-Day Check-In

At the 90-day mark, you will have a formal check-in with your manager to:
- Reflect on your first 3 months
- Discuss performance and integration into the team
- Confirm or adjust your role expectations and goals
- Discuss any questions or concerns

Probation completion is confirmed in writing by HR at the end of the probation period.

## Onboarding Buddy Program

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy — a peer (not your manager) who will help you navigate Call360 culture, answer informal questions, and make introductions. Buddies are volunteers and are matched based on role relevance.

## All Hands Meeting

Every third Tuesday of the month, Call360 hosts a company-wide All Hands. New joiners are introduced to the whole company. Previous recordings and notes are available in Confluence.
