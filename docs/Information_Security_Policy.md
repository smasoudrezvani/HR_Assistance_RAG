# Information Security Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.3 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Protecting Call360's information assets — including customer data, employee data, financial records, and intellectual property — is everyone's responsibility. This policy defines the minimum security standards that all employees must follow.

## Scope

This policy applies to all Call360 employees, contractors, and third parties with access to Call360 systems, networks, or data.

## Core Security Responsibilities

### 1. Protect Your Credentials
- Use strong, unique passwords for all work accounts (minimum 14 characters, mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
- Never share passwords or access credentials with anyone — including IT staff
- Use 1Password (company account) to generate and store all work passwords
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on all accounts that support it

### 2. Secure Your Devices
- Lock your computer when leaving your desk (Windows: Win+L; Mac: Ctrl+Command+Q)
- Enable full disk encryption on all company devices (configured by IT at setup)
- Do not disable or override security software installed by IT
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately to it@call360.com

### 3. Protect Company Data
- Do not store sensitive company data on personal devices or unauthorized cloud storage
- Use approved tools for file sharing (SharePoint, Confluence, Google Workspace if approved)
- Do not email sensitive documents to personal email addresses
- Shred physical documents containing sensitive information before disposal

### 4. Be Vigilant Against Phishing
- Do not click links or open attachments from unexpected or suspicious emails
- Verify unexpected requests for money transfers or credential changes via a separate communication channel
- Report suspected phishing to security@call360.com immediately

### 5. Use the VPN
- When accessing Call360 systems from outside the office (home, travel), connect to the Call360 VPN first
- VPN installation instructions are in Confluence → IT → Remote Access

### 6. Control Access
- Only access systems and data you need to perform your job
- Immediately notify IT when a colleague leaves the company or changes roles
- Do not share access with colleagues — everyone has their own credentials

## Incident Reporting

Security incidents must be reported **immediately** to security@call360.com or via the Security Incidents channel in Slack (#security-incidents). Examples of reportable incidents:
- Lost or stolen device
- Suspected malware or phishing
- Unauthorized access to systems
- Accidental exposure of customer or employee data

Do not attempt to investigate a security incident independently — report it immediately and let IT take it from there.

## Consequences of Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment. Serious incidents may also involve legal proceedings.
