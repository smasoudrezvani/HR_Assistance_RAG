# Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

Call360 issues company-owned devices to all permanent employees. As a general rule, employees should use company-issued devices for all work-related activities. This policy governs the limited scenarios where personal devices may be used.

## Call360's Default Position

Company-issued devices are the primary and preferred method of accessing Call360 systems. Employees should not routinely use personal devices for work purposes.

## When Personal Devices May Be Used

Personal devices may be used **on a limited basis** for:
- Receiving Slack notifications and responding to low-sensitivity messages
- Accessing the PeopleBase mobile app for leave requests
- Reviewing read-only documents (non-confidential)
- Joining video calls when a company device is temporarily unavailable (travel/emergency situations)

## Prohibited on Personal Devices

The following activities must **not** be performed on personal devices:
- Storing, editing, or transmitting sensitive company data or customer information
- Accessing internal systems beyond Slack and PeopleBase
- Processing personal data protected under GDPR or POPIA
- Writing or reviewing code on proprietary systems

## Security Requirements for Personal Devices Used for Work

If an employee regularly uses a personal device for any of the permitted activities above, that device must:
- Be protected by a screen lock with PIN/biometric authentication
- Have up-to-date operating system and security patches
- Not be jailbroken or rooted
- Have a reputable antivirus solution installed (personal cost)

## MDM (Mobile Device Management)

Call360 does not install MDM software on personal devices. Employees accept that when using their personal device to access company systems (e.g., Slack), those applications are subject to Call360's security policies.

## Remote Wipe

Call360 does not have the ability to remotely wipe personal devices. If a personal device is lost or stolen and was used to access Call360 systems, the employee must immediately notify IT and change all related passwords.

## Policy Acceptance

Use of personal devices for any work purpose constitutes acceptance of this policy.
