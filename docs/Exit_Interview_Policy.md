# Exit Interview Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Exit interviews provide valuable insight into the employee experience at Call360 — what is working well and what can be improved. This policy explains how exit feedback is collected and used.

## Who Participates

All employees leaving Call360 are invited to participate in an exit interview, regardless of the reason for departure (resignation, redundancy, end of contract, retirement).

Participation is **voluntary but strongly encouraged**. The feedback you provide is a genuine gift to your colleagues who remain.

## Two-Stage Process

### Stage 1: Exit Survey
All departing employees receive an automated exit survey via PeopleBase in their final month of employment. The survey covers:
- Overall experience at Call360
- Reasons for leaving
- Manager and team relationship
- Role and development satisfaction
- Culture and values alignment
- What Call360 does well
- What Call360 should improve

The survey takes approximately 10 minutes. Responses are anonymous unless the employee opts to share their name.

### Stage 2: Exit Interview (Optional)
Employees who wish to discuss their feedback in more depth are invited to schedule an exit interview with the HR Business Partner. This is a confidential, 30–45 minute conversation. It is optional.

The exit interview covers similar themes to the survey and provides space for more nuanced discussion.

## Confidentiality

- Individual exit responses are kept strictly confidential
- No identifying information is shared with the departing employee's manager
- Aggregated (anonymized) themes are shared periodically with leadership and managers to drive improvements

## How Feedback is Used

Call360's People & Culture team:
- Reviews exit survey trends quarterly
- Shares key themes and recommended actions with the leadership team
- Tracks whether actions taken in response to exit feedback result in improvements over time

Exit feedback is one of Call360's key listening mechanisms alongside the engagement survey, pulse checks, and 1:1 conversations.

## Scheduling an Exit Interview

Contact hr@call360.com to arrange an exit interview at a time convenient to you, ideally in your final 2 weeks.
