# Performance Review Process
**Call360 | Version 2.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360's performance review process exists to help employees understand how they are performing, what is expected of them, and how they can grow. Reviews are a conversation — not a judgment.

## Review Cycle

Call360 runs a **twice-yearly formal performance review**:

- **Mid-Year Review**: July (covering January–June)
- **Year-End Review**: December (covering July–December)

In addition, employees should have regular 1:1 check-ins with their manager at least monthly throughout the year.

## Components of the Review

### 1. Self-Assessment
All employees complete a self-assessment before their review meeting. This reflects on:
- Goals set at the start of the period and the degree of achievement
- Key contributions and accomplishments
- Areas for development
- Support needed from management

### 2. Manager Assessment
Managers evaluate the employee's performance against:
- Role expectations and competency framework
- OKRs / goals set at the start of the period
- Behavioral criteria (collaboration, communication, initiative, etc.)

### 3. Review Conversation
Employee and manager meet to discuss:
- Alignment between self-assessment and manager assessment
- Key feedback — strengths and development areas
- Career aspirations and growth opportunities
- Goal-setting for the next period

### 4. Rating

Performance is assessed on a 4-point scale:
- **Exceeds Expectations**: Consistently surpasses role requirements and delivers outstanding results
- **Meets Expectations**: Fully competent; delivers role requirements effectively and consistently
- **Partially Meets Expectations**: Delivers some requirements but has notable gaps; improvement is needed
- **Does Not Meet Expectations**: Significant performance concerns; a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) will be initiated

## Calibration

Before ratings are finalized, managers participate in a calibration session to ensure ratings are applied consistently across teams and departments.

## Outcomes

Performance reviews feed directly into:
- Salary review decisions (see Salary Review Process policy)
- Promotion eligibility
- Learning & Development planning
- Identification of high-potential employees

## Documentation

Reviews are documented in PeopleBase and accessible to the employee and their manager. Employees should receive written feedback within 5 business days of the review meeting.
