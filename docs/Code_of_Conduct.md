# Code of Conduct
**Call360 | Version 2.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

This Code of Conduct describes the standards of behavior expected from every Call360 employee, contractor, and representative. Acting with integrity is non-negotiable at Call360 — it protects our team, our customers, and our reputation.

## Core Principles

### 1. Integrity and Honesty
Be truthful in all communications — with customers, colleagues, partners, and leadership. Do not misrepresent facts, falsify records, or omit material information.

### 2. Respect and Inclusion
Treat everyone with dignity. Discriminatory behavior, bullying, or harassment of any kind is not tolerated. This applies in the office, during remote work, at company events, and on social media when representing Call360.

### 3. Confidentiality
Protect Call360's proprietary information including business strategies, customer data, financial information, and personnel matters. Do not share confidential information with unauthorized parties, even after leaving the company.

### 4. Conflicts of Interest
Avoid situations where personal interests could conflict — or appear to conflict — with Call360's interests. Disclose any potential conflict to your manager and HR promptly.

### 5. Compliance with Law
Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and company policies. This includes data protection laws (GDPR), labor laws, anti-bribery laws, and industry regulations.

### 6. Fair Dealing
Deal fairly with customers, suppliers, competitors, and colleagues. Do not take unfair advantage through manipulation, concealment, or abuse of privileged information.

### 7. Use of Company Resources
Use company resources — including devices, software, time, and budget — responsibly and for legitimate business purposes only.

## Reporting Concerns

If you witness or suspect a violation of this Code, you are encouraged to:

- Speak with your manager or the People & Culture team
- Contact the Ethics Hotline (confidential): ethics@call360.com
- Use the anonymous reporting form in PeopleBase

Call360 prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Consequences of Violations

Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. Serious violations may also be referred to law enforcement.
