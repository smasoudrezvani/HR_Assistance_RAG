# Background Check Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 conducts background checks on prospective employees to verify information provided during the hiring process and to fulfill our duty of care to customers, colleagues, and the business.

## Scope

Background checks apply to:
- All permanent employment offers
- Contractors and freelancers with access to sensitive customer data or financial systems (at Call360's discretion)

## What We Check

Standard checks for all roles:
- **Identity verification**: Confirmation of legal identity via government-issued ID
- **Right to work**: Verification of legal authorization to work in the country of employment
- **Employment history**: Verification of stated previous employment (last 3 employers or 5 years)
- **Education and qualifications**: Verification of stated degrees or certifications required for the role

Additional checks for specific roles:
- **Criminal background check**: Required for roles with access to financial systems, vulnerable populations, or significant trust
- **Credit check**: Required for finance or treasury roles (with candidate consent, in jurisdictions where permitted)
- **Professional license verification**: For roles requiring specific regulatory licenses

## Process

1. A conditional offer of employment is made pending successful background checks
2. The candidate provides consent and the required information via the secure background check portal (operated by **VerifyNow** — fictional provider)
3. VerifyNow conducts checks and reports to HR within 5–10 business days
4. HR reviews the report and discusses any discrepancies with the candidate before making a final decision
5. The candidate is informed of the final outcome

## Data Protection

- Background check data is processed under strict data protection protocols
- Results are accessed only by authorized HR personnel
- Data is retained for the duration of employment + 7 years for successful candidates; 12 months for unsuccessful candidates

## Adverse Findings

Not all adverse findings automatically disqualify a candidate. Each case is assessed individually, taking into account:
- The nature and age of any issue identified
- The relevance to the role being applied for
- Any explanation provided by the candidate

Candidates are given the opportunity to review and respond to any adverse findings before a final decision is made.

## Candidate Rights

Candidates have the right to:
- See the results of their background check
- Dispute inaccurate information with the provider
- Withdraw consent (which may affect the ability to proceed with the offer)
