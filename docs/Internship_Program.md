# Internship Program
**Call360 | Version 1.0 | Effective January 2026**

## About Call360 Internships

Call360 offers internships to students and recent graduates who want to gain real-world experience in a fast-growing international tech company. Interns work on meaningful projects, are treated as junior team members, and receive mentoring and feedback throughout their time with us.

## Duration and Timing

- **Duration**: 3–6 months (minimum 3 months to have meaningful impact)
- **Intake periods**: January and June/July (applications accepted on a rolling basis)
- **Hours**: Full-time (40 hours/week) or part-time (minimum 24 hours/week) by arrangement

## Eligibility

Internships are open to:
- Current bachelor's or master's students
- Recent graduates (within 6 months of graduation)

Call360 accepts interns in all departments depending on capacity and business need, including: Engineering, Data, Product, Marketing, Finance, and Operations.

## Compensation

Call360 pays interns a monthly stipend:
- **Amsterdam**: €600–€800/month depending on level and hours
- **Cape Town**: R5,000–R7,000/month
- **Remote**: Market-adjusted equivalent

Interns are not entitled to the standard employee L&D budget, referral bonus, or full leave entitlement.

## Leave and Absence

- Interns are entitled to 1.25 days paid leave per month worked
- Sick leave is handled on a case-by-case basis; interns should notify their manager as soon as possible
- Public holidays are observed as per the standard regional calendar

## What Interns Can Expect

- A clear project brief and defined deliverables from day 1
- A dedicated manager and day-to-day buddy
- Participation in team rituals (stand-ups, retros, team events)
- Bi-weekly feedback sessions with their manager
- A final presentation to their team and stakeholders at the end of the placement
- An end-of-placement reference letter

## Application Process

Applications are submitted via the Careers page at careers.call360.com. The process involves:
1. Application form and CV submission
2. Screening call with HR (15–20 min)
3. Technical or skills assessment (if applicable to the role)
4. Interview with the hiring manager (30–45 min)
5. Offer and enrollment

## Conversion to Full-Time

High-performing interns may be considered for permanent roles. This is not guaranteed and depends on business need and role availability at the time.
