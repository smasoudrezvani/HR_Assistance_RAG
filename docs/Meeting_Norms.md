# Meeting Norms and Culture
**Call360 | Version 1.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Why Meeting Norms Matter

Poorly run meetings waste time and energy. At Call360, we believe meetings should be purposeful, inclusive, and efficient. These norms help everyone get the most out of time spent together.

## Core Principles

1. **Only call a meeting if you need one** — many things can be resolved asynchronously in Slack or Confluence
2. **Every meeting needs an owner** — someone who sets the agenda, runs the meeting, and follows up
3. **Respect people's time** — start and end on time, every time
4. **Distribute ahead** — share agendas and pre-reads at least 24 hours before the meeting
5. **Document outcomes** — key decisions and action items must be documented after every meeting

## Meeting Scheduling Guidelines

- **Avoid scheduling meetings on Fridays** unless urgent (Call360 supports focus time on Fridays)
- **Check calendars before scheduling** — do not invite people to meetings where they have existing blocks
- **Keep meetings short**: default to 25 or 50 minutes (to allow transitions between back-to-back calls)
- **Only invite people who are essential** — large meetings slow down decisions

## Agenda Requirements

All meetings (except casual 1:1s) should have an agenda with:
- Purpose / goal of the meeting
- Topics to be discussed (with time allocated per topic)
- Pre-reads or preparation required

Add the agenda to the calendar invite or link to a Confluence document.

## Meeting Types and Defaults

| Meeting Type | Recommended Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1:1 (regular) | 30 min | Weekly or bi-weekly |
| Team stand-up | 15 min | Daily, async when possible |
| Project sync | 30–45 min | Focused; agenda required |
| Decision meeting | 30–60 min | Clear decision to be made |
| All Hands | 60 min | Monthly; company-wide |
| Retrospective | 60 min | Quarterly |
| Brainstorm / ideation | 60–90 min | With structured facilitation |

## Inclusion in Meetings

- Distributed teams: all meetings with remote participants should be held on video
- Use slides/screen sharing when relevant; ensure materials are accessible before the meeting
- Encourage everyone to contribute — explicitly invite quieter voices
- Use shared note-taking documents so all participants can follow along and contribute

## After the Meeting

Within 24 hours, the meeting owner should:
- Document key decisions in Confluence or the relevant project tool
- Assign action items with owners and due dates in Jira or the agreed tracking tool
- Send a follow-up summary to participants if needed
