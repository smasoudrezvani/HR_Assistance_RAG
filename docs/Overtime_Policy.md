# Overtime Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 respects employees' time and believes in sustainable working practices. This policy covers how overtime is defined, when it may be required, and how it is compensated.

## Definition of Overtime

Overtime is any time worked in excess of the employee's contracted hours as specified in their employment agreement. Standard contracted hours at Call360 are typically 40 hours per week.

## Philosophy on Overtime

Call360 does not expect or encourage regular overtime. Consistent overwork is a sign of under-resourcing or planning issues — not dedication. We aim to address workload through proper staffing, scope management, and realistic deadlines.

Occasional overtime may be necessary during:
- Product launches or major releases
- Customer escalations or incidents
- Year-end financial close or audits
- Other business-critical events

## Overtime by Region

### Netherlands (NL)
- Overtime is governed by the Dutch Working Hours Act (Arbeidstijdenwet)
- Employees may not work more than 12 hours per day or 60 hours per week
- Compensation for overtime is defined in the employment contract or applicable CAO (collective labor agreement)
- Time-off-in-lieu (TOIL) is the default arrangement; financial overtime pay requires explicit contract provision

### South Africa (ZA)
- Overtime is regulated by the BCEA
- Employees may not work more than 3 hours of overtime per day or 10 hours per week
- Overtime is compensated at **1.5× the regular hourly rate** or by time off in lieu
- Overtime on Sundays and public holidays is compensated at **2× the regular rate**

### Remote (Global)
- Remote employees working beyond contracted hours should discuss compensation with their manager
- TOIL is the default arrangement for occasional overtime
- Regular overtime patterns should be escalated to HR for workload review

## Process for Authorizing Overtime

1. Employees must obtain **prior written approval** from their manager before working overtime
2. Approved overtime is tracked in PeopleBase
3. TOIL should be taken within 30 days of the overtime worked (subject to manager scheduling)

## No Unauthorized Overtime Claims

Call360 will not compensate unauthorized overtime retroactively. Employees should never feel pressured to work beyond contracted hours without proper authorization and compensation.
