# Anti-Harassment and Anti-Bullying Policy
**Call360 | Version 2.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Policy Statement

Call360 is committed to providing a workplace free from harassment, bullying, and intimidation. Every employee has the right to work in an environment of mutual respect and professional dignity. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns, and third parties interacting with Call360 personnel.

## What is Harassment?

Harassment is any unwanted conduct related to a protected characteristic — including gender, race, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, national origin, pregnancy, or any other protected status — that has the purpose or effect of:

- Violating a person's dignity, or
- Creating an intimidating, hostile, degrading, humiliating, or offensive environment

### Examples of Harassment:
- Unwanted physical contact
- Offensive jokes, slurs, or derogatory comments
- Displaying offensive materials
- Excluding someone from work activities based on a protected characteristic
- Persistent unwanted attention

## What is Bullying?

Bullying is repeated unreasonable behavior directed toward a colleague that creates a risk to their health and safety.

### Examples of Bullying:
- Undermining a colleague's work or professional reputation without justification
- Shouting, screaming, or aggressive behavior
- Deliberately withholding information needed to perform a job
- Publicly humiliating or ridiculing a colleague
- Persistent nitpicking or micromanaging with no legitimate purpose

## Sexual Harassment

Sexual harassment is a specific form of harassment that includes:

- Unwanted sexual advances or requests for sexual favors
- Verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature
- Sharing inappropriate images or messages
- Creating a hostile environment based on sex or gender

## Reporting Procedure

Any employee who experiences or witnesses harassment or bullying should:

1. **Report immediately** — Contact your HR Business Partner or manager (if not the subject of the complaint)
2. **Document incidents** — Note dates, locations, witnesses, and what was said or done
3. **Use confidential reporting** — Anonymous reports can be submitted at ethics@call360.com

All complaints will be treated with confidentiality to the extent possible.

## Investigation Process

1. HR will acknowledge your complaint within 2 business days
2. A fair and impartial investigation will be conducted
3. Both parties will have the opportunity to present their account
4. A decision will be communicated within a reasonable timeframe
5. Appropriate action will be taken, proportionate to the findings

## No Retaliation

Retaliation against any person who makes a complaint in good faith, or who participates in an investigation, is strictly prohibited and is itself a disciplinary offense.

## Responsibilities

- **Employees:** Treat colleagues with respect; report concerns promptly
- **Managers:** Model respectful behavior; escalate concerns immediately
- **HR:** Handle all complaints fairly, promptly, and confidentially
