# Diversity, Equity and Inclusion (DEI) Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.3 | Effective January 2026**

## Our Commitment

At Call360, diversity, equity, and inclusion are not checkboxes — they are central to who we are and how we work. We are a global company with employees across multiple continents and cultures. Our diversity is our strength.

We are committed to:
- Building teams that reflect the diversity of the communities we serve
- Creating an environment where every person feels a genuine sense of belonging
- Addressing systemic inequities in our hiring, pay, and promotion practices
- Holding ourselves accountable through data and transparency

## Definitions

**Diversity** refers to the presence of differences among people — including gender, race, ethnicity, nationality, age, disability, sexual orientation, religion, socioeconomic background, neurodiversity, and more.

**Equity** means ensuring fair treatment, access, and opportunity — recognizing that different people may need different levels of support to achieve equal outcomes.

**Inclusion** means creating an environment where all individuals feel welcomed, respected, and able to contribute fully.

## Key Commitments

### Hiring
- Job descriptions are reviewed for inclusive language before posting
- Structured interviews with consistent scoring criteria minimize unconscious bias
- Diverse candidate slates are encouraged for all roles at L3 and above
- At least one diverse interviewer panel member is included in hiring for senior roles

### Pay Equity
- Annual pay equity audit to identify and close unjustified gaps by gender, ethnicity, or other protected characteristics
- Results shared with leadership and acted upon within the same review cycle

### Inclusive Culture
- All employees complete unconscious bias awareness training during onboarding
- Call360 actively celebrates cultural and religious diversity through internal events and the public holiday swap policy
- ERGs (Employee Resource Groups) are supported and encouraged — contact hr@call360.com to propose or join one

### Accommodations
- Call360 will make reasonable adjustments to support employees with disabilities or specific needs — contact hr@call360.com to discuss

## Accountability

- DEI KPIs are tracked and reported to the leadership team quarterly
- Employee sentiment on inclusion is measured in the annual engagement survey
- The People & Culture team is responsible for progressing Call360's DEI agenda

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that you believe is discriminatory or exclusionary, you can:
- Speak to your manager or HR Business Partner
- Email hr@call360.com
- Use the anonymous reporting form in PeopleBase

Call360 does not tolerate discrimination and will investigate all reports promptly.
