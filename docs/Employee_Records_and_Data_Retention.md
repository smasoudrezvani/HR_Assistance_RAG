# Employee Records and Data Retention Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

This policy explains what employee records Call360 maintains, how they are stored, who can access them, and how long they are retained.

## Types of Employee Records

Call360 maintains the following employee records:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Personal information | Name, DOB, address, emergency contacts |
| Employment records | Contract, job history, role changes, resignation letter |
| Compensation | Salary, bonus, payslips, benefits enrollment |
| Performance | Review forms, PIP documents, feedback |
| Leave and absence | Vacation requests, sick leave records, parental leave documentation |
| Disciplinary records | Written warnings, investigation records |
| Training and development | L&D activities, certifications, training completion |
| IT and access logs | System access and authentication logs |
| Health information | Sick certificates, occupational health recommendations |

## Where Records are Stored

All employee records are stored in:
- **PeopleBase (HRIS)**: Main repository for HR records
- **SharePoint (HR folder)**: Confidential documents requiring structured file storage
- **Payroll system**: Compensation and tax records

Physical documents (signed contracts, medical certificates) are stored securely in a locked filing system and scanned for digital storage.

## Access Controls

Access to employee records is strictly controlled:
- **Employees**: Can access their own records in PeopleBase
- **Direct managers**: Can view employment, performance, and leave records of direct reports
- **HR Business Partners**: Full access to records in their region
- **Finance**: Access to compensation and payroll-related records
- **Legal**: Access granted on a case-by-case basis

No other parties have access to individual employee records without explicit authorization.

## Data Retention Schedule

| Record Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Active employment records | Duration of employment + 7 years |
| Payroll and tax records | Duration of employment + 7 years |
| Disciplinary records | Duration of employment + 6 years |
| Performance reviews | Duration of employment + 5 years |
| Health/sick leave records | Duration of employment + 5 years |
| Recruitment records (unsuccessful candidates) | 12 months from application date |
| CCTV footage | 30 days |

Retention periods are based on legal requirements and may vary by jurisdiction.

## Employee Access to Their Own Records

Employees may request a copy of their personal records by submitting a Subject Access Request (SAR) to privacy@call360.com. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

## Disposal of Records

Records that have exceeded their retention period are deleted from digital systems and physically destroyed (shredded) in a secure and documented manner.
