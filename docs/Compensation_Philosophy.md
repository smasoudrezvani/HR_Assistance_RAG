# Compensation Philosophy
**Call360 | Version 1.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Our Approach to Compensation

Call360's compensation philosophy is built on three principles: **competitiveness**, **fairness**, and **transparency**.

We want every employee to be paid well for the value they bring — not to have to negotiate harder or wait for leverage to get what they deserve.

## Market Positioning

Call360 targets **total compensation at the 50th–75th percentile** of the relevant labor market for each role, location, and level. This is determined using annual benchmark data from reputable compensation survey providers.

We review and update our benchmarks every year in Q4 to ensure our salary bands remain current.

## What "Total Compensation" Includes

Total compensation at Call360 is made up of:

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| Base salary | Fixed monthly salary |
| Annual performance bonus | Up to 10–15% of base salary, based on company and individual performance |
| Learning & Development budget | 5% of annual salary, capped at €1,500 per year |
| Benefits | Health insurance, pension contribution, commute allowance |
| Equity (if applicable) | Available to senior/lead roles and above |

## Pay Equity

Call360 is committed to equal pay for equal work. We conduct an annual pay equity review to identify and remediate any unjustified pay gaps based on gender, ethnicity, or other protected characteristics. Results are shared with the leadership team and acted upon.

## Geographic Adjustment

Salaries for remote employees are adjusted based on the employee's country of residence, reflecting local cost of living and market rates. This approach enables us to hire globally while ensuring local competitiveness.

## Bonus

Call360 operates a discretionary annual bonus scheme:
- Company-wide pool is determined by financial performance
- Individual bonus is allocated based on performance rating and level
- Bonuses are paid in March following the performance year

## Transparency Commitment

Employees may ask HR for their salary band at any time. We encourage open conversations about pay and do not restrict employees from discussing compensation with colleagues.
