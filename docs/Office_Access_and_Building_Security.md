## Access Management

### Access Badges
All employees are issued a building access badge on their first day. This badge grants access to:
- Main office entrance
- Floors relevant to your team
- Common areas (kitchen, meeting rooms)

Badges must be worn visibly at all times when in the office and must not be transferred to or used by anyone else.

**If your badge is lost or stolen**: Report immediately to it@call360.com and the office manager so it can be deactivated.

### Visitors
All visitors must:
- Be registered in the visitor management system at reception before arrival
- Sign in at reception on arrival and receive a visitor badge
- Be accompanied by a Call360 employee at all times unless in designated visitor areas
- Never be left alone with access to secure office areas

### After-Hours Access
Access to the office outside of standard hours requires prior arrangement with the office manager. Standard after-hours access is 06:00–22:00. For access outside this window, contact facilities@call360.com.

## Physical Security Rules

- **Tailgating**: Never allow unknown individuals to follow you through secure doors
- **Unlocked areas**: Never leave sensitive documents, screens, or equipment visible and unattended
- **Clean desk**: At end of day, ensure your desk is clear of sensitive documents; lock away physical documents
- **Secure printing**: Retrieve printed documents promptly; use secure-print mode for confidential materials

## Emergencies

**Fire evacuation:**
- Follow the posted fire evacuation plan for your floor
- Muster point for Amsterdam: Herengracht canal side, east of the building
- Fire marshals are identified by orange vests; follow their instructions
- Do not use elevators during evacuation

**Security incidents:** Report any suspicious activity, tailgating attempts, or unauthorized persons immediately to the office manager or security@call360.com.

**Emergency numbers:**
- NL: 112
- ZA: 10111 (police) / 10177 (ambulance)
- Call360 emergency line: +31 20 000 0999

## Returning Access on Departure

All access badges must be returned to IT / office reception on the last working day.
