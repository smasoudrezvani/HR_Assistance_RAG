# Goal Setting and OKRs at Call360
**Call360 | Version 1.3 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

Call360 uses OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) as our primary goal-setting framework. OKRs create alignment between individual work and company strategy, and help everyone understand how their contribution matters.

## What Are OKRs?

**Objective (O):** A clear, inspiring, qualitative statement of what you want to achieve.
**Key Results (KRs):** 2–5 measurable outcomes that indicate whether the Objective has been achieved.

### Example OKR (Customer Support Representative):
- **Objective**: Deliver exceptional customer experiences in Q2
  - KR1: Achieve a customer satisfaction score (CSAT) of ≥90% across all tickets
  - KR2: Reduce average first response time to under 2 hours
  - KR3: Complete advanced support training module by end of quarter

## OKR Cadence

| Level | Frequency | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Company OKRs | Annual + Quarterly | Set in January and reviewed quarterly |
| Team OKRs | Quarterly | Aligned with company OKRs |
| Individual OKRs | Quarterly | Set during first 2 weeks of each quarter |

## How to Set Individual OKRs

1. Review your team's OKRs for the quarter
2. Draft 1–3 personal Objectives that connect to team goals
3. Define 2–5 Key Results per Objective that are measurable and time-bound
4. Discuss and agree OKRs with your manager during your first 1:1 of the quarter
5. Enter agreed OKRs into PeopleBase

## Scoring OKRs

At the end of each quarter, OKRs are scored:

- **1.0 (Full achievement)**: All key results met or exceeded
- **0.7 (Good progress)**: Most key results substantially met — this is considered success
- **0.5 (Partial progress)**: Some results met; significant gaps remain
- **0.0–0.4 (Insufficient progress)**: Little to no progress; discussion required

Scoring a 0.7 is the goal — OKRs set too low are not ambitious enough.

## OKRs Are Not Performance Evaluations

OKRs are a tool for focus and transparency. They are distinct from the formal performance review process, though OKR progress is considered during reviews. Employees should not be penalized for ambitious OKRs that come up short.

## Company OKRs

Company-level OKRs are shared with all employees at the All Hands meeting at the start of each quarter. Questions about strategy should be brought to the All Hands Q&A or to your manager.
