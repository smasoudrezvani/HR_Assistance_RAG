# Salary Review Process
**Call360 | Version 1.3 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

Call360 conducts a company-wide salary review once per year, typically in Q1 (January–February). This policy explains how the review works, what drives salary decisions, and what employees can expect.

## Review Cycle

- **Annual Salary Review**: Takes place each January, effective 1 March
- Mid-year salary adjustments are exceptional and require VP-level approval

## Factors Considered in Salary Review

Salary decisions are based on a combination of:

1. **Performance rating** from the most recent year-end review
2. **Position in salary band** — employees paid below market midpoint may receive higher increases
3. **Internal equity** — ensuring fairness and consistency across similar roles
4. **External market benchmarks** — Call360 benchmarks salaries annually against relevant market data
5. **Business financial performance** — the overall salary increase budget is set based on company results

## Salary Bands

Every role at Call360 is assigned to a salary band based on level and function. Salary bands are reviewed annually and anchored to the **50th–75th percentile** of relevant market benchmarks.

Employees may request a view of their salary band position from HR at any time.

## Eligibility

Employees are eligible for the annual salary review if they:
- Were employed before 1 October of the review year
- Have completed their probation period
- Are not currently on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

## How Increases Are Communicated

Salary review outcomes are communicated by managers in a 1:1 meeting, with a written confirmation letter provided via PeopleBase. Managers are given guidance and talking points by HR before these conversations.

## Off-Cycle Increases

In exceptional circumstances, off-cycle salary adjustments may be made for:
- Promotions (see Promotion and Career Growth policy)
- Significant market pay inequity identified by HR
- Retention cases approved by senior leadership

## Transparency

Call360 is committed to pay equity and transparency. While individual salaries are confidential, employees are encouraged to discuss compensation openly and to raise concerns about fairness with HR.
