# Whistleblower Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 is committed to ethical, lawful, and responsible business practices. We encourage all employees and stakeholders to speak up when they witness or suspect wrongdoing. This policy protects those who raise concerns in good faith.

## What to Report

This policy covers reports of serious concerns that go beyond a personal employment grievance. Examples include:

- Financial fraud or accounting irregularities
- Bribery, corruption, or conflicts of interest
- Serious data breaches or deliberate violations of privacy law
- Health and safety violations putting people at risk
- Environmental law violations
- Deliberate misrepresentation to customers, regulators, or authorities
- Any illegal activity involving Call360
- Serious breaches of Call360's Code of Conduct

## How to Report

### Internal Channels
- **Email**: ethics@call360.com (reviewed by a senior HR or Legal representative)
- **Anonymous web form**: Available in PeopleBase → Ethics → Report a Concern
- **Direct contact**: Call360's Legal Counsel or Chief People Officer

### External Channels
Where internal mechanisms have been used without satisfactory outcome, or where the concern involves senior leadership, concerns may be reported to the relevant national regulatory authority:
- **Netherlands**: Autoriteit Financiële Markten (AFM), Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (AP)
- **South Africa**: Companies Tribunal, Information Regulator, South African Police Service (SAPS)

## Anonymous Reporting

Anonymous reports are accepted and taken seriously. Note that anonymous reports may limit Call360's ability to investigate fully or to provide feedback to the reporter.

## Protections

Employees who raise concerns in good faith under this policy are protected from:
- Dismissal or threat of dismissal
- Demotion or negative performance reviews
- Harassment or victimization
- Any other form of retaliation

This protection applies even if the concern turns out to be unfounded, provided it was raised honestly and without malicious intent.

## False Reports

Making knowingly false, malicious, or misleading reports is a serious disciplinary offense.

## Investigation Process

All reports are investigated promptly and confidentially. The reporter will be kept informed of progress where possible and where anonymity is not an issue.
