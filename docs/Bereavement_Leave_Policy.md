# Bereavement and Special Leave Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.3 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 recognizes that employees may need time off to grieve and attend to personal matters following the death of a loved one, or for other significant life events. This policy outlines paid and unpaid special leave entitlements.

## Bereavement Leave Entitlements

The following paid leave is available to all Call360 employees, regardless of location:

| Relationship | Paid Leave Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Spouse, partner, or child | Up to 5 working days |
| Parent (including step-parent) | Up to 3 working days |
| Sibling | Up to 2 working days |
| Grandparent | 1 working day |
| Parent-in-law, child-in-law | 1 working day |
| Grandchild | 1 working day |
| Close friend or colleague (at manager's discretion) | Up to 1 working day |

Additional time off may be granted at the discretion of the manager and HR for travel, funeral arrangements, or administrative matters related to the bereavement.

## Other Special Leave Events

### Marriage or Civil Partnership Registration
- 0.5 day for registering the ceremony (banns or equivalent)
- 2 working days for the wedding or civil partnership ceremony itself

### Serious Illness of an Immediate Family Member
- Up to 3 days per year may be granted as emergency family responsibility leave to care for or support a critically ill immediate family member

### Court or Jury Duty
- Employees required to attend court as a witness or serve on jury duty are entitled to full pay for the duration of the legal obligation
- Proof of attendance may be required

### Emergency Situations
- Unplanned emergencies (e.g., home emergency, family crisis) may be accommodated with short-notice leave, subject to manager approval
- Up to 2 days per year may be granted under this category

## How to Notify

Notify your manager and HR as soon as you know you will need to take leave:
- Email: hr@call360.com
- Submit the leave request in PeopleBase under **Time Off → Special Leave**

Formal documentation (e.g., death certificate, invitation) may be requested but is handled sensitively.

## Extended or Additional Leave

If you need additional time beyond the standard entitlement, speak with your manager and HR. Options may include:
- Additional unpaid leave
- Taking vacation days
- A phased return to work

## Questions

Contact hr@call360.com. We are here to support you during difficult times.
