# Parental Leave Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 supports all parents in caring for their children during early childhood. This policy covers statutory parental leave entitlements and any enhancements provided by Call360 across all regions.

## What is Parental Leave?

Parental leave (distinct from maternity and paternity leave) is a period of leave that both parents may take to care for a child. It is typically available for use at any point up until the child reaches a certain age.

## Entitlements by Region

### Netherlands (NL)

Per Dutch law, each parent is entitled to **26 weeks of parental leave** in the first 8 years of their child's life.

- **First 9 weeks**: Must be taken in the first 12 months after birth. Paid at **70% of salary** via the UWV WAZO benefit.
- **Remaining 17 weeks**: Can be taken until the child's 8th birthday. These weeks are **unpaid**.
- Leave can be taken full-time, part-time, or spread out over time (subject to business needs).

### South Africa (ZA)

- Parental leave is included within the maternity and paternity leave entitlements as per the BCEA
- Employees seeking additional parental leave beyond statutory entitlement should discuss options with HR

### Remote (Global)

- Call360 offers **4 weeks of additional parental leave** (unpaid) that can be taken within the child's first 2 years, separate from the standard maternity/paternity entitlement
- This may be taken in a continuous block or split into 2-week blocks

## How to Apply

1. Notify HR at hr@call360.com at least **8 weeks in advance** of the intended leave start date
2. Complete the Parental Leave Request form in PeopleBase
3. Confirm return-to-work date at least 4 weeks before returning

## Employment Protections During Leave

- Employees on parental leave continue to accrue annual leave
- Employees are entitled to return to the same role (or an equivalent role at the same level) upon return
- Salary and benefits continue as per your employment contract during paid leave periods

## Phased Return

Employees returning from extended parental leave may request a phased return-to-work schedule. Discuss options with your manager and HR.
