# Email and Internal Communication Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Clear, professional, and secure communication underpins effective collaboration at Call360. This policy provides guidelines for email and internal messaging tools.

## Primary Communication Channels

| Tool | Used For |
|---|---|
| Slack | Real-time messaging, team collaboration, quick decisions |
| Microsoft Outlook | Formal external communication, contracts, official notices |
| Confluence | Documentation, knowledge base, policies |
| Microsoft Teams | Video calls (if applicable per team) |

## Email Guidelines

### Professional Standards
- Use your call360.com email address for all business correspondence
- Use clear, professional language and a proper greeting/close
- Respond to emails within **1 business day** for internal messages, **same or next business day** for external
- Use a professional email signature (template available in Confluence → IT → Signature)

### Appropriate Use
- Use email for external communications and formal internal matters
- Do not use email to share sensitive customer data without encryption
- Do not forward confidential information to personal email addresses

### Mailing Lists and Distribution Groups
- Do not subscribe the entire company to mailing lists without Communications team approval
- Reply-all should be used sparingly; avoid reply-all chains with large groups

## Slack Guidelines

### Channel Etiquette
- Use the appropriate channel for your message (avoid cross-posting)
- Keep #general for company-wide relevant announcements only
- Use threads to keep conversations organized
- Use status to indicate availability (in a meeting, away, focus time)

### Response Expectations
- During working hours: respond to Slack messages within **2 hours**
- Outside working hours: no expectation to respond; use "Do Not Disturb" settings
- Use Slack's scheduled send feature to avoid sending messages outside colleagues' working hours

### Privacy
- Slack messages on company accounts may be reviewed by authorized administrators for compliance purposes
- Do not send highly sensitive data (passwords, personal health info) via Slack — use 1Password or secure email instead

## Monitoring and Privacy

Call360 may monitor email and communication tool usage for security, legal compliance, and business continuity purposes, within the bounds of applicable law. Employees will be informed of any such monitoring where required by law.
