# IT Equipment Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.4 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

This policy covers the provision, use, and return of IT equipment provided by Call360 to employees.

## Standard Equipment

All permanent employees receive the following standard equipment on their first day:
- **Laptop**: MacBook Air (M-series) — default for most roles; Windows laptop available upon request for specific technical requirements
- **Headset**: Noise-cancelling USB or Bluetooth headset
- **Laptop charger**

Additional equipment may be provided based on role:
- External monitor (available upon request for office-based roles)
- Docking station (for hybrid employees regularly working at the Amsterdam office)
- Ergonomic peripherals (keyboard, mouse, monitor stand) — see Ergonomics Policy

## Remote Employees

Remote employees receive their standard equipment shipped to their home address before their start date. A one-time **home office setup allowance** of €500 is also available to purchase additional equipment not provided directly (see Work From Home Equipment Allowance policy).

## Equipment Ownership and Responsibilities

- All equipment remains the **property of Call360**
- Employees are responsible for the safe and careful use of equipment
- Equipment must be used primarily for professional purposes
- Personal use is permitted to a reasonable degree and must not compromise security
- Employees must not install unauthorized software on company devices
- Equipment must not be used by any person other than the assigned employee

## Loss, Damage, or Theft

- Report any loss, theft, or significant damage immediately to it@call360.com and your manager
- Call360 will replace equipment in cases of theft or accidental damage, up to one incident per year
- Negligent damage (e.g., dropping a laptop while under the influence, deliberate misuse) may result in a partial cost recovery from the employee
- In case of theft, a police report is required

## Security Requirements

All company equipment must:
- Be encrypted (FileVault on Mac, BitLocker on Windows — enabled by IT at setup)
- Have a screen lock that activates after 5 minutes of inactivity
- Run Call360's required security software (antivirus, MDM agent)
- Be kept with software up to date (updates pushed by IT)
- Never be left unattended in public places

## Return of Equipment

On an employee's last working day, all equipment must be returned to IT in full working condition:
- Office employees: return directly to IT at the office
- Remote employees: IT will coordinate collection or shipping

Failure to return equipment may result in the value being deducted from the final paycheck.
