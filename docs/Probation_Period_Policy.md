# Probation Period Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

All new permanent employees at Call360 are subject to a probation period during which both the employee and the company assess whether the working relationship is a good fit.

## Duration

- **Standard probation period**: 3 months
- **Extended probation** (exceptional cases, e.g., senior roles or formal underperformance concerns): up to 6 months total, communicated in writing before the end of month 3

Probation periods are specified in the employment contract.

## During Probation

### What We Expect
During probation, employees are expected to:
- Demonstrate competence in their core responsibilities
- Build positive relationships with their team and stakeholders
- Engage actively with onboarding and learning activities
- Exhibit behaviors consistent with Call360's values

### What to Expect From Us
Call360 will:
- Provide a clear role description and performance expectations from day 1
- Assign an onboarding buddy
- Conduct structured check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days
- Provide feedback throughout — not just at the end

### 30-Day Check-In
A brief check-in with the manager to ensure the employee is settling in, has the tools they need, and understands their initial priorities.

### 60-Day Check-In
A more substantive conversation covering:
- Early performance feedback
- Any areas requiring development or support
- Updated goals for the remainder of the probation period

### 90-Day / Probation Review
A formal review meeting where the manager assesses whether probation should be:
- **Confirmed**: Employment continues; a confirmation letter is issued by HR
- **Extended**: Where there are concerns that are addressable with more time
- **Not confirmed**: Where the role is not a good fit; notice period applies (see Termination Policy)

## Leave and Benefits During Probation

- Annual leave accrues from day 1
- During probation, employees must obtain manager approval before taking leave
- The L&D budget is accessible after 2 months of employment
- All other benefits apply from day 1 unless otherwise stated in the employment contract

## Notice Period During Probation

During probation, the notice period for both parties is typically **1 week**, as specified in the employment contract.
