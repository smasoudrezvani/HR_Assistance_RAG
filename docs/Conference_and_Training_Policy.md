# Conference and External Training Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Attending conferences and external training programs is a valuable way for Call360 employees to stay current in their field, build networks, and bring new ideas back to the team. This policy outlines how to request approval and how costs are covered.

## Funding

Conference and training costs are covered from the employee's annual L&D budget (see Learning and Development Budget policy). For events exceeding the individual L&D cap, additional funding may be available from the team training budget — discuss with your manager.

## What is Covered

When attending an approved conference or external training, the following costs may be reimbursed from your L&D budget:
- **Registration fees** — conference ticket, course enrollment
- **Travel** — economy flights or train tickets booked in advance (see Travel Policy)
- **Accommodation** — reasonable hotels at standard rate (see Travel Policy)
- **Per diem** — meals and incidentals at €45/day (NL), R400/day (ZA), or equivalent

Luxury travel, business class flights, or premium hotels require senior management pre-approval.

## Approval Process

1. Identify the conference or training
2. Prepare a brief justification: What will you learn? How will it benefit your role and Call360?
3. Discuss with your manager and get written approval
4. Book early to minimize costs
5. Submit expenses with receipts via PeopleBase within 30 days of returning

## Knowledge Sharing

After attending a conference or significant training, employees are expected to:
- Share key takeaways with their team in a brief (15–30 min) presentation or written summary
- Post a short summary in the relevant Slack channel (#learning-and-development)
- Share any materials (presentations, notes) in Confluence

This ensures the wider organization benefits from your learning investment.

## Paid Time for Attendance

Up to **2 paid study days per calendar year** may be used for external training or conference attendance during working hours. Additional days require manager approval and are unpaid.

## International Conferences

For international conferences requiring flights:
- Book at least 4 weeks in advance when possible
- Use economy class for flights under 6 hours
- Accommodation: up to €150/night (NL standard) or local equivalent
