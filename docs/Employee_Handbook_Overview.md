# Employee Handbook Overview
**Call360 | Version 3.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Welcome to Call360

Welcome to Call360! We are an international telecommunications company connecting people across borders through affordable, reliable calling solutions. Our mission is to make the world feel smaller — one call at a time.

This handbook is your go-to guide for understanding how we work, what we stand for, and what you can expect as a member of the Call360 team.

## About This Handbook

This handbook applies to all Call360 employees across all locations including our Amsterdam headquarters, Cape Town office, and remote employees worldwide. It is a living document and is updated periodically. The most recent version is always available in PeopleBase (our HRIS) and on the internal Confluence.

**Note:** This handbook is a guide, not a rigid rulebook. We trust our employees to use good judgment. When in doubt, speak with your manager or the People & Culture team.

## What's Covered

This handbook covers the following areas:

- **Employment Basics** — contracts, probation, working hours, location
- **Compensation & Benefits** — salary, bonuses, insurance, pension
- **Time Off & Leave** — vacation, sick leave, parental leave, special leave
- **Performance & Development** — reviews, feedback, career growth, L&D budget
- **Workplace Conduct** — code of conduct, anti-harassment, confidentiality
- **IT & Security** — equipment, software, passwords, data privacy
- **Policies & Procedures** — expenses, travel, disciplinary process, offboarding

## Our Values

Everything at Call360 is guided by four core values:

1. **Connect** — We build bridges, not walls — with customers, colleagues, and communities.
2. **Own It** — We take responsibility for our work and outcomes.
3. **Grow** — We are curious learners who never stop improving.
4. **Care** — We treat everyone with respect, empathy, and fairness.
