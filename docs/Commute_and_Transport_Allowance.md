# Commute and Transport Allowance Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

Call360 provides commute support to office-based and hybrid employees to make getting to the office easier, more affordable, and more sustainable.

## Netherlands (NL) — Commuting Options

NL employees can choose between two commuting support options:

### Option 1: Public Transport Card (NS Business Card)
- Call360 provides an **NS Business Card** for commuting by public transport (train, tram, bus, metro, OV-fiets)
- This card covers the cost of commuting directly — no expense submission required
- Personal use is not permitted
- If you use your OV-fiets as part of a train commute, this is included and not claimed separately

### Option 2: Mileage Reimbursement
- Employees commuting by personal car, bike, e-bike, or scooter may claim **€0.23 per kilometer** (subject to annual tax authority adjustment)
- Capped at **40 km one-way** (80 km round trip per commute day)
- Submit monthly via PeopleBase → Expenses with date, mode of transport, and distance

**Important**: Do not claim mileage on days when you use an NS Business Card. Only one option per commute day.

## South Africa (ZA)

ZA employees receive a **commute allowance** as part of their compensation package, as agreed in their employment contract. This is included in the monthly payroll.

## Remote (Global)

- Remote employees do not receive a commute allowance
- A **home internet allowance** of €30/month is provided to offset the cost of home internet for work use (see Benefits Overview)

## Claiming the NL Mileage Reimbursement

1. Track commute days and distances (one-way distance from home to office)
2. At month-end, log in to PeopleBase → Expenses
3. Submit a monthly mileage claim with:
   - Each commute day
   - Mode of transport
   - One-way distance (km)
   - Calculated total (distance × 2 × €0.23 × number of days)
4. Submit by the 5th of the following month

**Example**: If you live 12 km from the office and cycle in 15 times per month:
12 km × 2 × €0.23 × 15 = €82.80/month

## Sustainability Note

Call360 encourages sustainable commuting. Cycling, e-biking, and public transport are all reimbursed and contribute to personal wellbeing and reduced environmental impact.
