# Work From Home Equipment Allowance Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 provides all employees with a company laptop and essential peripherals. For employees working fully remote or in a hybrid arrangement, an additional home office equipment allowance is available to support a safe, comfortable, and productive work environment.

## Allowance Details

- **Amount**: One-time allowance of **€500** (or local equivalent) per employee
- **Eligibility**: All permanent employees with a remote or hybrid work arrangement
- **When available**: From the employee's first day of employment

The allowance may be claimed any time during employment, but it is a **one-time benefit** (not annual).

## What the Allowance Can Be Used For

| Eligible | Not Eligible |
|---|---|
| External monitor | Replacement for company-issued laptop |
| Ergonomic chair or desk | Gym equipment |
| Standing desk or sit-stand converter | Personal decorations |
| Laptop stand, keyboard, mouse | Unrelated household items |
| Webcam or ring light | Food, beverages |
| Desk lamp | Clothing |
| Noise-cancelling headphones (if not already provided) | Items already covered by IT equipment provision |

## How to Claim

1. Purchase the item(s) yourself
2. Keep receipts
3. Submit via PeopleBase → Expenses → WFH Allowance category
4. Include receipt and a brief description of the item
5. Reimbursement is processed with the monthly payroll run

Claims must be submitted within 6 months of the purchase date.

## Internet Allowance (Monthly)

In addition to the one-time equipment allowance, remote and hybrid employees receive a **€30/month internet contribution** to offset home broadband costs. This is paid automatically via payroll — no monthly claim required.

## Ownership

Items purchased using the WFH equipment allowance are owned by the employee. Call360 does not require return of these items upon departure.

This differs from company-issued equipment (laptop, headset) which remains the property of Call360.
