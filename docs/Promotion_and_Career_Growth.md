# Promotion and Career Growth Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.4 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 is committed to investing in the growth of every employee. This policy explains how promotions work, what criteria are considered, and how employees can take ownership of their career development.

## Career Framework

Call360 uses a career leveling framework with 5 levels across all roles:

| Level | Title Examples |
|---|---|
| L1 | Junior / Associate |
| L2 | Mid-level / Individual Contributor |
| L3 | Senior |
| L4 | Lead / Principal / Manager |
| L5 | Director / Head of |

Each level has defined expectations for scope, skills, ownership, and impact. Level descriptions are available in PeopleBase under **Development → Career Framework**.

## Promotion Criteria

Promotions are not automatic or tenure-based. To be promoted to the next level, an employee must:

1. **Consistently operate at the next level** — performance at the current level is not sufficient
2. **Demonstrate readiness** across the key competencies for the higher level
3. **Have manager endorsement** — the manager must actively sponsor the promotion case
4. **Pass calibration** — promotions are reviewed and calibrated by a cross-functional HR & leadership group

## Promotion Process

1. **Manager nominates** the employee for promotion before the promotion cycle deadline
2. **Employee prepares a promotion packet** with evidence of impact, feedback, and competency examples
3. **Calibration review** by HR and peer managers
4. **Decision communicated** to employee and manager
5. **Compensation adjustment** takes effect from the start of the next pay period

Promotion cycles run **twice per year** (aligned with the performance review cycle in July and December).

## Self-Initiated Career Conversations

Employees are encouraged to proactively discuss career goals with their manager. You do not need to wait for a formal review cycle to have these conversations.

Use the following structure in your 1:1:
- Where are you now? (current level and strengths)
- Where do you want to go? (short-term and long-term aspirations)
- What support do you need? (skills gaps, stretch projects, mentorship)

## Lateral Moves

Promotions are not the only form of career growth. Internal transfers to a different team or function at the same level are encouraged when they align with personal development goals. Employees can explore open internal roles in PeopleBase under **Jobs → Internal Openings**.
