# Employee Referral Program
**Call360 | Version 1.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

Call360's Employee Referral Program (ERP) rewards employees who help us find great new colleagues. You know our culture, you know your network — help us grow with people who will thrive here.

## Referral Bonus

A successful referral results in a **€1,000 gross** one-time bonus paid to the referring employee.

**Payment timing**: The bonus is paid after the referred employee has completed their **full probation period** (typically 3 months), provided:
- The referred employee has not resigned
- The referred employee has not been asked to leave
- The referring employee is still employed by Call360

## What Counts as a Referral?

A referral is a candidate who:
- Has not previously applied to Call360 in the last 12 months
- Is applying for a permanent employment position (not intern, freelancer, or contractor)
- Was actively introduced to Call360 through you — not someone who found the job independently

## How to Refer Someone

1. Check the open roles on Call360's careers page
2. Reach out to your contact and get them excited about the role
3. Submit their CV or LinkedIn profile via the Referral Form in PeopleBase or by emailing hr@call360.com with the subject line: **[REFERRAL] Candidate Name – Role Title**
4. HR will confirm receipt and keep you updated on the process

You don't have to have spoken to the candidate about the role in advance, but it helps!

## Which Referrals Don't Qualify?

- Referrals from **hiring managers referring candidates for their own team** do not qualify
- In-house recruiters are not eligible for referral bonuses
- Referrals of interns, consultants, or freelancers do not qualify
- If the same candidate is referred by multiple employees, the bonus goes to the first referral submitted

## Confidentiality

Referral submissions are confidential. A candidate will not be told who referred them unless you choose to disclose this yourself.

## Tax

The €1,000 gross referral bonus is subject to applicable income tax and social security contributions.
