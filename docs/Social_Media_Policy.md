# Social Media Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Social media is a powerful channel for Call360 and its employees. This policy helps employees understand how to use social media responsibly — both as a representative of Call360 and as an individual.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees posting on social media platforms in any capacity where Call360 is mentioned or implied — including LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Reddit, and any other public platform.

## Personal Social Media Use

You are free to maintain personal social media accounts. When doing so:

### Do:
- Feel free to mention that you work at Call360
- Share publicly available Call360 news or content that you're proud of
- Express personal opinions clearly as your own (e.g., "In my personal view...")
- Be professional and respectful in all communications

### Don't:
- Share confidential company information, financial data, or unreleased product details
- Speak on behalf of Call360 unless you are explicitly authorized to do so
- Post anything that could damage Call360's reputation or the reputation of colleagues
- Harass, disparage, or mock customers, partners, or competitors
- Share screenshots of internal tools, systems, or communications without HR approval

## Authorized Company Spokespersons

Only the following roles are authorized to post content on behalf of Call360's official channels:
- Marketing & Communications team members
- CEO and C-level executives (with Communications team support)
- Designated community managers

If you are asked to post on behalf of Call360 in an unofficial capacity, confirm authorization with hr@call360.com first.

## Recruitment and Employer Branding

Employees are encouraged to share Call360's job openings on LinkedIn and to represent Call360 positively as an employer. This is valuable for employer branding.

## Responding to Criticism

If you see negative commentary about Call360 online and are not an authorized spokesperson:
- Do not respond or engage
- Share the link with hr@call360.com or Communications so it can be addressed appropriately

## Violations

Serious violations — such as sharing confidential data or making discriminatory statements — may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
