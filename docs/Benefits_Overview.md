# Benefits Overview
**Call360 | Version 2.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Introduction

Call360 offers a competitive benefits package designed to support your health, financial wellbeing, and work-life balance. Specific entitlements vary by region and are detailed in the policies linked below.

## Core Benefits — All Employees

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| Annual leave | 20–25 days depending on region |
| Sick leave | Paid sick leave per regional policy |
| Parental leave | Maternity, paternity, adoption, and parental leave |
| Learning & Development budget | 5% of annual salary, capped at €1,500/year |
| Employee Assistance Program (EAP) | Free confidential counselling and support |
| Employee referral bonus | €1,000 gross per successful hire |
| Public holiday swap | Swap up to 2 public holidays per year |

## Netherlands (NL) Benefits

- **Health insurance**: Call360 contributes to a group health insurance policy via ZorgVerzekering Groep (an imaginary provider); details shared at onboarding
- **Pension**: 7% pension supplement included in gross salary for self-managed pension contributions
- **Commute allowance**: NS Business Card (public transport) or €0.23/km mileage reimbursement (up to 40 km one-way)
- **Annual leave**: 25 days
- **Internet allowance**: €30/month for home internet (remote or hybrid employees)

## South Africa (ZA) Benefits

- **Medical aid**: 100% employer-funded for salaries below R180,000; 75%/25% split for mid-range; 50%/50% for higher earners
- **Retirement fund**: Employer contribution via PensionSure (fictional provider)
- **Annual leave**: 15 days
- **Family responsibility leave**: Up to 3 days per year

## Remote (Global) Benefits

- **Home office setup allowance**: One-time €500 allowance for new hires (see Work From Home Equipment Allowance policy)
- **Internet allowance**: €30/month contribution toward home internet costs
- **Annual leave**: 20 days (includes public holidays)
- **Annual travel allowance**: €500 toward visiting a Call360 office once per year

## Annual Benefits Review

Call360 reviews its benefits package annually in Q4. Employees are surveyed for feedback via PeopleBase to ensure the package remains competitive and relevant.
