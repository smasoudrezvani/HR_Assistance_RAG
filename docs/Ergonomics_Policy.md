# Ergonomics Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Poor ergonomics causes preventable discomfort, injury, and reduced productivity. Call360 is committed to ensuring all employees — whether in the office or working from home — have access to ergonomically suitable working setups.

## Office Ergonomics

Call360's offices are equipped with:
- Adjustable desks (sit-stand options are available in both the Amsterdam and Cape Town offices)
- Ergonomic task chairs
- External monitors, keyboards, and mice at all fixed workstations

If your workstation does not feel comfortable or you experience discomfort, contact facilities@call360.com to request an ergonomic assessment.

## Home Office Ergonomics

Employees working remotely or in a hybrid arrangement are responsible for maintaining an ergonomically suitable home workspace. Basic requirements:

### Desk and Chair
- Chair should support the lower back; feet should be flat on the floor
- Desk height should allow forearms to rest horizontally when typing
- A lumbar support cushion or adjustable chair can be purchased using the WFH Equipment Allowance

### Screen Setup
- Top of the screen should be at or just below eye level
- Screen should be roughly arm's length from your face
- Use the WFH Equipment Allowance to purchase a monitor stand or laptop stand if needed

### Keyboard and Mouse
- Wrists should be straight and relaxed when typing
- Ergonomic keyboards and mice are available through the WFH Equipment Allowance

### Lighting
- Work in a well-lit environment
- Avoid glare on the screen — position your screen perpendicular to windows
- A desk lamp can be purchased via the WFH allowance

## Ergonomic Assessments

Any employee experiencing musculoskeletal discomfort related to their work setup may request an ergonomic assessment:
- **In the office**: Contact facilities@call360.com
- **Remote workers**: Request a virtual ergonomic consultation via hr@call360.com

Call360 will arrange a virtual assessment by a qualified ergonomics advisor at no cost to the employee.

## Equipment Requests

Office employees may request additional ergonomic equipment from IT or Facilities. Remote employees may use the WFH Equipment Allowance to purchase ergonomic equipment.

Requests for specialized medical-grade ergonomic equipment (e.g., due to a diagnosed condition) will be assessed by HR on a case-by-case basis.

## Breaks and Micro-Movement

Regardless of setup quality, regular movement breaks are essential:
- Take a short break every 45–60 minutes of screen time
- Stand, stretch, or walk briefly at least twice per half-day
- Consider a standing desk or sit-stand converter to alternate postures
