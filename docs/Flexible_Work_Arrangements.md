# Flexible Work Arrangements Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 values flexibility as a cornerstone of employee wellbeing and productivity. This policy outlines the types of flexible work arrangements available, how to request them, and what expectations apply.

## Types of Flexible Work Arrangements

### 1. Flexible Start/End Times
Employees may adjust their start and end times within a window of 07:00–20:00, provided:
- Core collaboration hours (09:00–14:00 CET) are covered
- The manager is informed and agrees
- Scheduled meetings and deliverables are not impacted

### 2. Compressed Work Week
Employees may work a full 40-hour week across 4 days (e.g., Mon–Thu) with approval from their manager. This arrangement is reviewed every 3 months.

### 3. Part-Time Work
Employees may request to reduce their working hours. Part-time arrangements affect compensation, leave accrual, and L&D budgets on a pro-rata basis. See the Part-Time Work Policy for details.

### 4. Job Sharing
Two part-time employees may share a single full-time position. Both employees share responsibilities, communication, and accountability. See the Job Sharing Policy for details.

### 5. Temporary Adjustments
Short-term flexibility (e.g., caring for a sick family member, personal appointments) may be accommodated informally by agreement with your manager, without a formal policy change.

## Eligibility

All permanent employees are eligible to request flexible arrangements. Eligibility is subject to:
- Role requirements and team impact
- Performance track record
- Length of service (minimum 3 months recommended before requesting structural changes)

Some operational roles (e.g., customer support with fixed shift schedules) have limited flexibility options.

## How to Request

1. Discuss your preferred arrangement with your manager
2. Complete the Flexible Work Request Form in PeopleBase
3. HR and your manager will review within 5 business days
4. If approved, a written agreement will be added to your employment file

Requests will be evaluated fairly and without bias. Denial of a request will be accompanied by a written explanation.

## Review and Adjustments

Flexible arrangements are reviewed every 6 months. Either the employee or the company may request a modification with 4 weeks' notice if business needs change significantly.

## Manager Responsibilities

Managers should:
- Evaluate requests fairly and consistently across team members
- Consider the impact on team collaboration and customer commitments
- Document all approved arrangements
