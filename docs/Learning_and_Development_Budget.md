# Learning and Development Budget Policy
**Call360 | Version 2.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 believes in continuous learning. Every employee has access to a personal Learning & Development (L&D) budget to invest in their professional growth.

## Budget Entitlement

- **Amount**: 5% of annual gross salary, capped at **€1,500 per calendar year**
- **Pro-rata**: If you join mid-year, the budget is calculated pro-rata from your start date
- **Available after**: 2 months of employment
- **Rollover**: The budget does **not roll over** to the following year — use it or lose it

## What the Budget Can Be Used For

The L&D budget is intended for activities that develop your professional skills and capabilities directly relevant to your role. Examples include:

- Online courses and certifications
- Professional workshops and seminars
- Business-relevant books (digital or physical)
- Industry conferences (including travel and accommodation, within annual cap)
- Coaching or mentoring (work-related)
- Language courses (if relevant to your role)
- Travel to another Call360 office for knowledge sharing

The budget is **not** intended for general personal development unrelated to your role (e.g., hobbies, personal fitness, general interest courses).

## Part-Time Employees

- **Working 0–23 hours per week**: 50% of the standard budget (max €750/year) and 1 study day
- **Working 24+ hours per week**: Full budget and 2 study days

## Study Days

Employees may take up to **2 paid study days per year** for approved L&D activities. Additional study days beyond 2 are unpaid and require manager approval.

## Approval Process

- **Under €100**: Verbal approval from your manager is sufficient
- **€100 or more**: Written approval (email) from your manager is required — attach this when submitting your expense

### Steps:
1. Identify the learning activity
2. Discuss with your manager in a 1:1
3. Obtain written approval (if required)
4. Complete the activity
5. Submit expense via PeopleBase (Expenses section)

## 2-Year Education Agreement

Employees may request to spend two years' L&D budget in a single year for a significant learning investment (e.g., a professional certification program). This requires:
- Senior management approval
- A signed Education Agreement with a clawback clause: if you leave within 12 months of the activity, up to 50% may be recouped

## Clawback

If you resign or are terminated for cause having spent more than 50% of your annual L&D budget, Call360 reserves the right to recoup up to 50% of the amount spent.

## Team Learning

Managers may organize team training sessions funded from a shared L&D team budget. This is separate from individual allocations.
