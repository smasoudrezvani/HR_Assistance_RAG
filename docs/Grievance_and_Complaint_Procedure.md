# Grievance and Complaint Procedure
**Call360 | Version 1.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 wants all employees to be able to raise concerns about their work, working conditions, or relationships with colleagues and management without fear. This procedure provides a fair, structured process for resolving grievances promptly and fairly.

## What is a Grievance?

A grievance is a formal concern or complaint raised by an employee about:
- The behavior of a manager or colleague
- Terms and conditions of employment
- Perceived unfair treatment or discrimination
- Working conditions or environment
- Application of company policy

For concerns about harassment or bullying specifically, please refer to the Anti-Harassment Policy.

## Step 1: Informal Resolution

Before raising a formal grievance, employees are encouraged to try to resolve the issue informally:
- Speak directly with the person involved (if appropriate and safe to do so)
- Speak with your manager (if they are not involved in the issue)
- Speak informally with the HR Business Partner for guidance

Many issues can be resolved at this stage quickly and without lasting conflict.

## Step 2: Formal Grievance

If the informal approach does not resolve the issue, you may raise a formal grievance by:
1. Submitting a written complaint to hr@call360.com or via PeopleBase → Grievances
2. Including: your name, nature of the grievance, relevant dates, people involved, and any steps already taken

HR will acknowledge your complaint within **2 business days**.

## Step 3: Investigation

HR will appoint an impartial investigator (typically the HR Business Partner or an external HR advisor if HR is implicated). The investigation will:
- Review all relevant evidence and documents
- Interview the complainant, the respondent, and any relevant witnesses
- Be conducted confidentially — only those who need to know will be informed
- Be completed within **15 business days** where possible

Both parties may be accompanied by a colleague or trade union representative during formal meetings.

## Step 4: Decision and Outcome

Following the investigation, a written outcome will be provided to both parties, including:
- Findings of the investigation
- Any actions to be taken
- Any remedies or adjustments proposed

If the grievance is upheld, appropriate action will be taken. This may include mediation, management intervention, policy changes, or disciplinary action against the respondent.

## Step 5: Appeal

If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may appeal in writing within **5 business days** of receiving the decision. Appeals are reviewed by a more senior manager or HR Director not previously involved in the case.

## No Retaliation

Raising a genuine grievance is a protected act. Retaliation against any employee who has raised a grievance in good faith is a disciplinary offense.
