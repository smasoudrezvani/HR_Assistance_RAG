# Dress Code Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Philosophy

Call360 trusts employees to dress appropriately for their role and the context of their work. We don't have a rigid dress code — we have common sense guidelines that reflect our casual but professional culture.

## General Standard: Smart Casual

Our default dress code is **smart casual**. This means neat, clean clothing that is appropriate for a professional environment. Think: jeans with a clean shirt, casual trousers, smart sneakers.

## Context-Specific Guidelines

### In the Office (Regular Day)
- Smart casual is appropriate on most days
- Avoid overly casual items such as athletic wear, torn clothing, or beachwear
- Comfort is encouraged — most people dress how they feel best in a relaxed, modern office

### Customer or Partner Meetings (In-Person)
- Step up to **business casual** for external meetings: neat trousers or skirt, collared shirt or blouse
- When in doubt, ask your manager what the client's culture is

### Video Calls
- A presentable top half is expected for external calls with partners, clients, or senior stakeholders
- Internal calls are more relaxed — professional judgment applies

### Company Events
- Dress code for events (e.g., team dinners, company offsites) is communicated in the event invitation
- All Call360 events are inclusive environments — dress comfortably and appropriately for the context

## What is Not Appropriate

At any time in the workplace or in client-facing contexts:
- Clothing with offensive slogans, images, or messaging
- Beachwear or gym-only attire during regular work hours
- Clothing that could be considered culturally insensitive or demeaning

## Religious or Cultural Attire

Call360 fully respects and accommodates all forms of religious or cultural dress. If you ever feel that a dress-related expectation conflicts with your cultural or religious practice, speak with HR — we will always find a respectful solution.

## Remote Employees

Video call norms apply. On non-video days, dress as you see fit.
