# Offboarding Process
**Call360 | Version 1.3 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

While we're sorry to see you go, we want your departure from Call360 to be smooth, respectful, and professional. This guide explains the steps involved in leaving Call360.

## Step 1: Confirming Your Resignation

1. Schedule a meeting with your manager to discuss your resignation
2. Submit a signed resignation letter to both your manager and hr@call360.com
3. HR will confirm your notice period and last working day in writing
4. Agree with your manager on how and when to communicate the news to the team

## Step 2: Notice Period

Your contractual notice period begins the day your resignation is formally acknowledged by HR. The standard notice period at Call360 is:
- **Probationary employees**: 1 week
- **Employees 0–1 year**: 1 month
- **Employees 1–5 years**: 1 month (NL) / 4 weeks (ZA)
- **Employees 5+ years**: 2 months (NL) / as per contract (ZA)

Annual leave may not be accrued or taken during the notice period unless agreed with HR.

## Step 3: Handover

Work with your manager to complete a thorough handover of all responsibilities:
- Document key processes and active projects in Confluence
- Transfer ownership of files to designated colleagues in SharePoint or Google Drive
- Brief your successor or covering colleagues
- Complete all outstanding deliverables or clearly communicate status
- Update your manager on open tasks and blockers

Confirm with your manager when the handover is complete.

## Step 4: Returning Equipment

Before your last day, return all company-owned equipment:
- Laptop (with charger and accessories)
- Headset, monitor, keyboard, and any other peripherals
- Office access card or keys
- Any physical documents or confidential materials

Remote employees: HR will coordinate return shipping. Do not post equipment without instructions.

IT will disable your accounts and access on your final working day.

## Step 5: Exit Survey and Interview

All departing employees are invited to complete:
- An **exit survey** (sent automatically via PeopleBase in your final month)
- An optional **exit interview** with the HR Business Partner

Your feedback is valuable and helps Call360 improve. Responses are treated confidentially.

## Final Pay

Your final paycheck will include:
- Salary for days worked in your final month
- Payout of accrued but unused vacation days (as per regional policy)
- Any outstanding expense reimbursements

## Post-Departure Obligations

After leaving Call360, you remain bound by:
- Confidentiality obligations under your employment contract or NDA
- Non-compete and non-solicitation clauses (if applicable)

Thank you for your contribution to Call360. We wish you every success in your next chapter.
