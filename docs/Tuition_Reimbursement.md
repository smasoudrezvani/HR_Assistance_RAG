# Tuition Reimbursement Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 supports employees pursuing formal academic qualifications or professional certifications that are relevant to their current or future role at Call360. This policy outlines eligibility, reimbursement limits, and conditions.

## Eligibility

To be eligible for tuition reimbursement, employees must:
- Have completed at least **6 months of service** with Call360
- Be in good standing (not on a Performance Improvement Plan or under disciplinary review)
- Pursue a qualification directly relevant to their role or career growth at Call360
- Obtain prior written approval from their manager and HR before enrolling

## What is Covered

- **Tuition fees**: Registration, course, and exam fees for accredited programs
- **Study materials**: Required textbooks and course materials

**Not covered**: Application fees, late fees, personal equipment, accommodation for full-time study programs, or non-accredited courses.

## Reimbursement Limits

The tuition reimbursement budget is separate from the standard L&D budget:
- Up to **€2,000 per calendar year** for part-time academic programs (while remaining employed full-time)
- Requests above this limit require VP-level approval

## Process

1. Research the program and confirm eligibility with HR
2. Submit a Tuition Reimbursement Request in PeopleBase with:
   - Program name and provider
   - Relevance to your role (written justification)
   - Cost breakdown and schedule
3. Obtain approval from manager and HR
4. Enroll and begin study
5. Submit receipts in PeopleBase upon completion of each module or semester

## Study Time

Formal academic study is generally conducted outside working hours. Employees may use up to 2 study days per year (shared with the standard L&D study day entitlement) for exam preparation.

## Conditions and Clawback

- Reimbursement is paid after successful completion of each period of study (not upfront)
- If an employee **leaves Call360 within 12 months** of receiving reimbursement, they must repay:
  - 100% if they leave within 6 months
  - 50% if they leave within 6–12 months
- This is documented in a written Education Agreement signed before reimbursement begins
