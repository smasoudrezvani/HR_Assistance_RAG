# Expense Reimbursement Policy
**Call360 | Version 2.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 reimburses employees for reasonable, pre-approved business expenses incurred on behalf of the company. This policy defines what qualifies for reimbursement, how to claim it, and applicable limits.

## General Principles

- Expenses must be **business-related** and **pre-approved** (unless below the no-approval threshold)
- Always choose the most **cost-effective option** that meets the business need
- Receipts are required for all claims above €10 / R100
- Expenses must be submitted within **30 days** of being incurred

## Approval Thresholds

| Amount (€ equivalent) | Approval Required |
|---|---|
| Under €50 | No prior approval needed; submit with receipt |
| €50–€250 | Manager approval (via email) before spending |
| Over €250 | Manager + Finance approval before spending |

## Common Reimbursable Expenses

### Travel
- Economy flights (booked at least 14 days in advance where possible)
- Train / bus tickets
- Taxi or rideshare to/from airports for work travel
- Reasonable ground transportation during business trips

### Accommodation
- Hotel: up to €150/night (Amsterdam / Cape Town); up to €120/night (other)
- Airbnb or similar is permitted if more cost-effective

### Meals (Per Diem)
- **NL / Europe**: €45/day (or itemized receipts)
- **ZA**: R400/day (or itemized receipts)
- **Other regions**: Apply local equivalent agreed with manager in advance

### Office Supplies
- Items needed for home office use that are not provided by IT (see WFH Equipment Allowance for one-time allowance)

### Conferences and Training
- See Conference and Training Policy — funded from L&D budget

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursed:
- Alcohol (unless it is a group team dinner pre-approved by a manager at VP level or above)
- Personal entertainment, clothing, or gifts
- Traffic fines or parking tickets
- Personal trips or activities during business travel
- Expenses that exceed policy limits without prior approval

## How to Submit an Expense

1. Collect all receipts
2. Log in to PeopleBase → Expenses
3. Submit each expense with:
   - Date and amount
   - Business purpose
   - Project or budget code (if applicable)
   - Receipt (upload photo or PDF)
   - Manager approval email (for expenses requiring it)
4. Submit by the 5th of the month for same-month processing

Reimbursements are paid with the monthly payroll run.
