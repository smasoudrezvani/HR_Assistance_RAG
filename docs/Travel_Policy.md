# Business Travel Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.3 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

This policy governs business travel for Call360 employees, covering booking, accommodation, travel standards, and safety.

## Approval

All business travel requires prior approval:
- **Domestic travel** (within the employee's home country): manager approval
- **International travel**: manager + department head approval
- **Travel over €1,000 total cost**: Finance sign-off required in addition

Submit travel requests through the Travel Request Form in PeopleBase at least 7 days before travel where possible.

## Booking Travel

Travel should be booked through the company's preferred booking channel (TravelConnect — internal link in Confluence) or via the employee's local travel arrangement process. Where possible, book at least 14 days in advance to minimize costs.

### Flights
- **Domestic / regional flights**: Economy class
- **Long-haul flights over 8 hours**: Premium Economy may be approved for senior roles (L4+) — requires Finance approval
- Book direct routes where cost difference is minor

### Rail
- Standard class tickets; first class may be booked for trains over 3 hours if the cost difference is minimal and pre-approved

### Accommodation
- Book hotels at reasonable rates (see Expense Reimbursement Policy for nightly limits)
- Prefer options near the meeting location to minimize internal transport costs

### Ground Transport
- Use public transport or rideshare apps (Uber, Bolt) where practical
- Rental cars: economy class; requires manager approval
- Taxi from airport: acceptable when arriving at late hours or with heavy equipment

## Meals and Allowances

Per diem allowances apply during business travel (see Expense Reimbursement Policy). Keep receipts for all meal expenses.

## Travel Safety

- Register your travel in PeopleBase → Travel (so HR has a record)
- Check government travel advisories for your destination (e.g., gov.nl, dfa.gov.za)
- Call360 carries business travel insurance for all employees on approved trips — details in Confluence → HR → Benefits → Travel Insurance
- In case of emergency abroad: contact the Emergency Travel Helpline: +31 20 000 0111 (24/7)

## Personal Travel Combined with Business Travel

If you extend a business trip for personal reasons:
- The personal portion of flights is your own expense
- Accommodation during personal days is your own expense
- You must inform HR in advance if the personal extension affects your attendance at work

## Sustainability

Call360 encourages employees to minimize the environmental impact of business travel:
- Consider video conferencing as an alternative to short trips
- Choose train over plane for journeys under 4 hours
- Carbon offset options are available when booking through TravelConnect
