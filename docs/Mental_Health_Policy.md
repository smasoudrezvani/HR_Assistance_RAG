# Mental Health and Wellbeing Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Our Commitment

Call360 recognizes that mental health is just as important as physical health. We are committed to creating a workplace culture where employees feel safe discussing mental health challenges, where stigma is actively challenged, and where appropriate support is always available.

## Key Principles

1. **Mental health is health.** We treat mental health challenges with the same seriousness and compassion as physical illness.
2. **Confidentiality is guaranteed.** Employees who disclose mental health challenges to HR or management will have their privacy respected.
3. **Support before performance management.** If performance is being affected by mental health, our first step is to explore support — not discipline.
4. **No stigma.** Managers are trained to respond supportively and to never make employees feel judged for mental health disclosures.

## What Support is Available

### Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
All employees have free access to the Call360 EAP (MindBridge EAP — fictional provider) which provides:
- Confidential counselling sessions (up to 6 per year per issue)
- 24/7 crisis support line
- Financial and legal advice
- Access via phone, online, or mobile app

See the EAP Policy for full details.

### Sick Leave for Mental Health
Employees may take sick leave for mental health reasons without having to disclose the specific nature of their condition. This is treated identically to physical illness.

### Flexible Arrangements
Employees experiencing mental health challenges may request temporary flexible working arrangements (reduced hours, adjusted responsibilities) while they recover. This is coordinated with HR and the manager.

### Return-to-Work Support
Employees returning after a mental health-related absence will receive a structured return-to-work plan developed collaboratively with HR, their manager, and (if applicable) the occupational health doctor.

## Manager Responsibilities

Managers play a key role in creating a psychologically safe environment. They are expected to:
- Complete mental health awareness training (available in the Learning Hub)
- Check in regularly with team members — especially during high-pressure periods
- Respond supportively and non-judgmentally if an employee discloses a mental health challenge
- Escalate to HR early if they are concerned about a team member's wellbeing

## Wellbeing Initiatives

Call360 supports employee wellbeing through:
- Flexible working arrangements
- Regular company-wide wellbeing check-ins
- Access to the EAP
- Healthy snacks and beverages in the office
- Recharge Fridays (Q3 initiative — see Culture & Values Overview)
