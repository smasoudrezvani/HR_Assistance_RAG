# Visitor Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

This policy ensures that visitors to Call360 offices are managed safely, professionally, and in compliance with our security and data protection obligations.

## Types of Visitors

- **Business visitors**: Partners, clients, candidates, suppliers, consultants
- **Personal visitors**: Family or friends of employees (permitted under specific conditions)
- **Service providers**: Maintenance, IT vendors, cleaners (managed by Facilities)

## Pre-Registration

All business visitors must be pre-registered:
1. The Call360 employee hosting the visit submits visitor details via the Visitor Registration Form in PeopleBase or by emailing facilities@call360.com **at least 24 hours before the visit**
2. Provide: visitor name, company, purpose of visit, expected arrival and departure time, hosting employee

Visitors will receive a confirmation email with building access instructions.

## Arrival Procedure

On arrival, visitors must:
1. Report to reception and state their name and who they are visiting
2. Present valid photo ID (passport, driving licence, or national ID card)
3. Sign the visitor log and acknowledge Call360's visitor guidelines (NDA may be required for certain visits)
4. Receive a visitor badge — to be worn visibly at all times during the visit

## Hosting Responsibilities

The Call360 employee hosting the visit is responsible for:
- Meeting the visitor at reception promptly
- Accompanying the visitor at all times in the office
- Ensuring the visitor does not access areas beyond those needed for the meeting
- Ensuring the visitor does not access company systems unless authorized
- Returning the visitor badge to reception at the end of the visit
- Signing out the visitor in the visitor log

## Sensitive Areas

Visitors must not be brought into:
- Server rooms or IT infrastructure areas
- Finance and HR areas with visible employee data
- Any area holding confidential customer or product information

## Personal Visitors

Personal visitors (family, friends) may visit employees briefly (e.g., lunch) subject to:
- Prior approval from the employee's manager
- Sign-in at reception
- Supervision by the employee at all times

Personal visitors may not be brought to workstations or any area with sensitive information visible.

## Incident Reporting

Report any visitor-related security concerns (e.g., a visitor accessing unauthorized areas, refusing to sign out) immediately to security@call360.com.
