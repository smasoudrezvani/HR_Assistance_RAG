# Data Privacy Policy (Employee)
**Call360 | Version 2.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 is committed to protecting the privacy of employee personal data in compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and equivalent legislation in other jurisdictions where we operate.

## What Personal Data We Collect

As your employer, Call360 collects and processes the following categories of employee personal data:
- Identity information: name, date of birth, national ID / passport number
- Contact information: home address, phone number, personal email
- Employment data: job title, team, employment contract, salary, performance reviews
- Payroll data: bank account details, tax information
- Leave and absence data
- Health information (relevant to sick leave and occupational health obligations)
- IT system access logs and activity data on company devices

## Legal Basis for Processing

Call360 processes employee personal data on the following legal bases:
- **Contract**: Data necessary to fulfill employment obligations
- **Legal obligation**: Data required by law (tax, labor, health & safety)
- **Legitimate interest**: Data necessary for business operations, security, and fraud prevention
- **Consent**: For optional data or uses beyond employment requirements (e.g., social media photos)

## How We Use Your Data

Employee data is used for:
- Payroll processing and benefits administration
- Performance management and HR administration
- Compliance with legal and regulatory obligations
- Internal directory and organizational planning
- IT security and access management

## Data Retention

Employee data is retained for as long as required by applicable law and legitimate business need. Typically:
- Active employee data: retained for the duration of employment
- Post-employment data: retained for up to 7 years (legal compliance)

## Your Rights

As an employee, you have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data held by Call360
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure**: Request deletion of data (subject to legal retention obligations)
- **Restriction**: Limit certain processing of your data
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a portable format

Submit a data request to: privacy@call360.com

## Data Security

Call360 implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect employee data, including access controls, encryption, and regular security audits.

## Third-Party Sharing

Employee data is shared only with trusted third-party processors (e.g., payroll providers, health insurers) under appropriate data processing agreements. Data is never sold to third parties.
