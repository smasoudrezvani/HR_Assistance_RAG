# Termination Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

Termination of employment at Call360 may occur for a range of reasons including resignation, redundancy, end of fixed-term contract, dismissal, or retirement. This policy sets out the principles and process that apply in all cases.

## Types of Termination

### 1. Resignation (Employee-Initiated)
See the Resignation Process policy for full details. Standard notice periods apply.

### 2. Dismissal for Cause
Dismissal following a disciplinary process. See the Disciplinary Process policy.

### 3. Dismissal Without Notice (Summary Dismissal)
In cases of gross misconduct (e.g., theft, fraud, assault), Call360 may terminate employment immediately without notice, following a fair investigation and hearing.

### 4. Redundancy
When a role is made redundant due to business restructuring, the following applies:
- Employees are given as much advance notice as possible
- Statutory and contractual redundancy pay applies (per regional law)
- Alternative roles within Call360 will be explored before a redundancy is confirmed
- Outplacement support may be provided at Call360's discretion

### 5. End of Probation
If an employee does not meet the required standards during their probation period, employment may be terminated with reduced notice (typically 1 week) following a documented performance discussion.

### 6. Fixed-Term Contract Expiry
Fixed-term contracts expire on the agreed end date. If not renewed, the employment ends automatically. Appropriate notice is given where required by law.

## Notice Periods on Termination

| Tenure | NL Notice | ZA Notice |
|---|---|---|
| Probation | 1 week | 1 week |
| 0–1 year | 1 month | 1 month |
| 1–5 years | 1 month | 4 weeks |
| 5+ years | 2 months | Per contract |

Payment in lieu of notice (PILON) may be made by mutual agreement.

## Final Settlement

On termination, the final paycheck will include:
- Salary for all days worked
- Accrued and unused vacation days (paid out per regional law)
- Any outstanding reimbursable expenses
- Bonus (if due and not forfeited)

Final payment is made within the payroll cycle following the last working day, or as required by local law.

## Legal Compliance

All terminations are handled in compliance with applicable labor law in the employee's jurisdiction. Employees are encouraged to seek independent legal advice if they have concerns about the process.
