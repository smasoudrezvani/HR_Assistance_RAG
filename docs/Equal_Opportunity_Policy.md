# Equal Opportunity Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Policy Statement

Call360 is an equal opportunity employer. We are committed to ensuring that all employment decisions — including recruitment, promotion, training, compensation, discipline, and termination — are made based on merit, ability, and business need, free from discrimination.

## Protected Characteristics

Call360 does not discriminate on the basis of any protected characteristic, including but not limited to:
- Gender or gender identity
- Race, ethnicity, or national origin
- Age
- Disability (physical or mental)
- Sexual orientation
- Religion, faith, or belief
- Pregnancy or maternity
- Marital or family status
- Socioeconomic background
- Neurodiversity

This applies to all aspects of employment, including:
- Job advertising and recruitment
- Selection and hiring
- Induction and training
- Performance reviews and promotions
- Compensation and benefits
- Working arrangements and conditions
- Disciplinary procedures
- Redundancy and termination

## Positive Action

Where there is evidence of under-representation of a group at certain levels or in certain areas, Call360 may take positive action measures — such as targeted outreach in recruitment or mentoring programs — to address imbalances. This is consistent with applicable law and does not mean giving an unfair advantage to any individual.

## Responsibilities

**All employees** must:
- Treat all colleagues, candidates, customers, and partners with equal respect
- Not discriminate against or harass any person on the basis of a protected characteristic
- Report any suspected discrimination or inequality to HR promptly

**Managers** must:
- Apply objective, consistent criteria in all employment decisions
- Complete bias awareness training annually
- Escalate any concerns about discrimination or inequity in their team to HR

**HR** must:
- Monitor employment data for patterns of inequality
- Investigate all equal opportunity complaints fairly
- Ensure policies and practices remain compliant with applicable law

## Complaints and Investigation

If you believe you have been treated unequally or discriminated against:
1. Raise the matter with your HR Business Partner or email hr@call360.com
2. HR will investigate impartially and in confidence
3. Appropriate remedial action will be taken if discrimination is found

See also: Grievance and Complaint Procedure policy.
