# Public Holiday Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

Call360 observes public holidays in each country of employment. This policy explains which holidays apply to employees, how they affect leave balances, and how Call360 handles situations where work is required on a public holiday.

## Public Holidays by Region

### Netherlands (NL)
NL employees observe all official Dutch public holidays as paid days off. These do not count against the vacation balance.

Standard Dutch public holidays include (but may vary year to year):
- New Year's Day
- Good Friday (varies)
- Easter Monday (varies)
- King's Day (27 April)
- Liberation Day (5 May — every 5 years as official holiday, but recognized by Call360 annually)
- Ascension Day (varies)
- Whit Monday (varies)
- Christmas Day (25 December)
- Second Christmas Day (26 December)

### South Africa (ZA)
ZA employees observe all official South African public holidays as paid days off. These do not count against vacation leave.

Standard South African public holidays include:
- New Year's Day, Human Rights Day, Good Friday, Family Day, Freedom Day, Workers' Day, Youth Day, National Women's Day, Heritage Day, Day of Reconciliation, Christmas Day, Day of Goodwill

### Remote (Global)
Remote employees are entitled to observe the public holidays of their country of residence. **These days are drawn from the employee's 20-day annual leave balance.** A full schedule is available in PeopleBase at the start of each year.

## Working on Public Holidays

If a business need requires an employee to work on a public holiday:
- **NL employees**: Entitled to the day off in lieu or enhanced pay as per Dutch law or employment contract
- **ZA employees**: Paid at double the regular daily rate
- **Remote employees**: May take an alternative day off in lieu within 30 days

All public holiday work must be agreed in advance with the employee's manager.

## Public Holiday Swap

Employees may be eligible to swap a standard public holiday for a culturally or personally significant alternative. See the Public Holiday Swap Policy for details.
