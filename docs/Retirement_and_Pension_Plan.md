# Retirement and Pension Plan
**Call360 | Version 1.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

Call360 believes that planning for retirement is important. This policy outlines the pension and retirement contribution arrangements available to employees by region.

## Netherlands (NL)

### Current Arrangement
Call360 does not operate a collective company pension scheme (pensioenregeling) at this time.

Instead, **7% of gross salary is included as a pension supplement** in the monthly gross salary. Employees are responsible for arranging their own pension savings plan (e.g., via a bank or insurer such as ABN AMRO, ASN, or similar).

### Recommended Approach
HR recommends employees:
- Open a personal pension savings account (lijfrente or banksparen)
- Contribute the 7% supplement monthly
- Seek advice from a financial advisor if needed

Call360 will review the feasibility of a collective pension arrangement as part of the annual benefits review.

## South Africa (ZA)

### Provident Fund
Call360 ZA provides a **provident fund** through **PensionSure** (fictional provider). Contributions are:
- **Employee contribution**: 5% of monthly gross salary
- **Employer contribution**: 5% of monthly gross salary

Contributions are deducted from payroll each month and transferred to the fund.

Employees may increase their personal contribution voluntarily, up to the annual tax-free limit as specified by SARS.

New ZA employees are enrolled automatically during onboarding.

## Remote (Global) Employees

Remote employees are responsible for making their own retirement savings arrangements in accordance with the laws and tax rules of their country of residence. Call360 does not currently provide a global pension contribution.

Employees are encouraged to consult a local financial advisor.

## Leaving Call360

Upon termination of employment:
- NL employees retain their personal pension savings independently
- ZA employees' provident fund balances are managed by PensionSure and transferred to a preservation fund or new employer scheme as elected by the employee
