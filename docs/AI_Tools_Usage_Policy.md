# AI Tools Usage Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

AI tools offer significant productivity and quality benefits for Call360 employees. This policy ensures we use these tools responsibly, securely, and in compliance with data protection obligations.

## Approved AI Tools

Call360 maintains an approved AI tools list in Confluence. As of 2026, approved tools include:

| Tool | Use Case |
|---|---|
| Call360 AI Assistant (internal) | Internal Q&A, drafting, research |
| Approved code assistants | Engineering (see IT list for approved tools) |
| AI meeting note taker (see policy) | Meeting transcription and summaries |

Use of AI tools not on the approved list requires prior approval from IT and Information Security via the Software Request Form in PeopleBase.

## What You Can Use AI Tools For

AI tools may be used to:
- Draft documents, emails, reports, and summaries
- Brainstorm and ideate
- Research general knowledge topics
- Write, review, or explain code (using approved coding assistants)
- Transcribe and summarize meetings (using the approved AI note taker)
- Translate content

## What You Must NOT Do With AI Tools

**Never input the following into external or unapproved AI tools:**
- Customer personal data (names, contact details, financial information)
- Employee personal data
- Confidential business strategies, financial forecasts, or M&A information
- Proprietary source code or unreleased product details
- Passwords or security credentials
- Legal advice or privileged attorney-client communications

When in doubt, do not paste the content into an external AI tool.

## AI-Generated Content

AI-generated output must always be reviewed before use:
- **Check for accuracy**: AI tools can hallucinate facts, dates, names, and sources
- **Check for bias**: AI can reflect or amplify biases — review with a critical eye
- **Review for quality**: AI output is a starting point, not a final product
- **Do not present AI output as your own original research** without review and attribution where appropriate

## AI Meeting Notes

AI meeting notes are governed by a separate policy (AI Meeting Notes Policy). In short: AI transcription must not be used in sensitive or confidential meetings (HR discussions, performance reviews, legal matters, etc.).

## Data and Privacy

When using approved tools that process data:
- Only use company accounts (not personal accounts) for work-related AI tool use
- Ensure any data processed complies with Call360's Data Privacy Policy
- Report any suspected data leakage to security@call360.com immediately

## AI and Decision-Making

AI tools must not be used as the sole basis for decisions affecting employees (e.g., performance assessments, hiring decisions). Human judgment must remain central to all people decisions.
