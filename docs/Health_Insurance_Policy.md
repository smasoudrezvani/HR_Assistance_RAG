# Health Insurance Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

Call360 supports the health of all employees through regional health insurance arrangements. Coverage details vary by location and are outlined below.

## Netherlands (NL)

### Group Health Insurance
Call360 facilitates access to a group health insurance plan through a partner provider. This provides:
- Basic statutory health insurance (basisverzekering) — mandatory for all NL residents
- Group discount rate on supplemental coverage (aanvullende verzekering)

Employees are responsible for paying their own health insurance premiums directly. Call360 does not currently pay premiums on behalf of NL employees but provides a gross salary contribution to help offset healthcare costs.

**Note**: Health insurance in the Netherlands is a personal obligation; everyone living and working in the Netherlands must have basic coverage.

### Sick Leave Coverage
Dutch law provides income protection during illness. See the Sick Leave Policy for full details.

## South Africa (ZA)

### Medical Aid
Call360 provides medical aid coverage through **HealthBridge** (fictional provider) for all permanent ZA employees.

Coverage contribution tiers:
- **Up to R180,000 p.a. salary**: 100% employer-funded
- **R180,001–R600,000 p.a. salary**: 75% employer / 25% employee
- **Above R600,000 p.a. salary**: 50% employer / 50% employee

Medical aid is **compulsory for all ZA permanent employees**.

New joiners will be contacted by HR to select their coverage level within the first 2 weeks of employment.

### Gap Cover
Call360 does not currently provide gap cover insurance. Employees may arrange this personally at their own cost.

## Remote (Global) Employees

Remote employees are responsible for their own health insurance arrangements in line with the laws of their country of residence. Call360 includes a **€50/month health insurance allowance** in remote employees' gross compensation to help offset these costs.

## Claiming Health-Related Expenses

Health-related expenses (e.g., doctor visits, prescription medication) are not reimbursed by Call360 beyond the contribution structures above, unless covered by a specific role-based risk benefit.
