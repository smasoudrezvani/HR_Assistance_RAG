# Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
**Call360 | Version 1.3 | Effective January 2026**

## What is the EAP?

The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) is a free, confidential support service available to all Call360 employees and their immediate family members. It provides professional assistance for personal, emotional, and work-related challenges.

Call360 partners with **MindBridge EAP** (fictional provider) to deliver this service.

## What the EAP Covers

The EAP offers support across a wide range of areas, including:

### Mental Health
- Anxiety, stress, and burnout
- Depression and mood disorders
- Work-related mental health challenges
- Grief and loss

### Relationships and Family
- Relationship difficulties
- Parenting challenges
- Family conflict
- Domestic difficulties

### Financial and Legal Advice
- Debt counselling
- Budgeting assistance
- Basic legal guidance (employment, family, housing)

### Workplace Support
- Conflict with colleagues or management
- Managing change or uncertainty at work
- Returning to work after illness

### Crisis Support
- 24/7 crisis line for acute mental health emergencies

## How to Access the EAP

The EAP is completely **free** to all employees and their immediate household members.

**Access methods:**
- **Phone**: +31 20 000 0000 (24/7, multilingual)
- **Online portal**: eap.call360.com (login details shared at onboarding)
- **App**: Available on iOS and Android — search "MindBridge EAP"

Sessions are completely confidential. Call360 does not receive any individual-level data. Only aggregate, anonymized usage statistics are shared.

## Confidentiality

All contacts with the EAP are strictly confidential. The EAP provider will not share any information about your use of the service with Call360, your manager, or HR — unless you give explicit consent, or there is a risk to life.

## Number of Sessions

Each employee is entitled to **up to 6 free counselling sessions** per year per issue. After 6 sessions, the EAP counsellor will help you identify ongoing support options.

## Mental Health at Call360

Call360 actively promotes a culture where mental health is taken seriously. If you are struggling, please:
- Speak with your manager or HR in confidence
- Take sick leave when needed — mental health is health
- Use the EAP proactively, not just in crisis
