# Freelancer and Contractor Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

This policy governs the engagement of external freelancers and contractors by Call360. It ensures consistent standards, compliance with employment law, and proper classification of workers.

## Definitions

**Freelancer / Independent Contractor**: A self-employed individual or company engaged by Call360 for a specific project, deliverable, or fixed period, who is not on Call360's payroll and does not receive employee benefits.

**Contractor (via Agency)**: A worker provided through a staffing agency who works at Call360 but is employed by the agency.

**Consultant (Firm)**: A professional services firm engaged for a specific scope of work.

## When to Engage a Contractor

Contractors may be engaged for:
- Specialized skills not available in-house
- Short-term projects with a defined scope and end date
- Surge capacity during high-demand periods
- Specific technical or creative deliverables

Contractors should not be used as a long-term substitute for permanent hiring.

## Engagement Process

1. The hiring manager identifies the need and obtains budget approval
2. HR reviews the engagement for worker misclassification risk
3. Legal reviews and issues a Service Agreement / SOW (Statement of Work)
4. The contractor completes onboarding steps required by IT (access setup, security training)
5. Invoices are submitted and approved via Finance

All contractors must sign:
- Service Agreement or SOW
- Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
- Data Processing Agreement (if handling personal data)

## Classification Compliance

Misclassifying employees as freelancers carries significant legal and financial risk. Call360 applies the following tests:
- Does Call360 control **how** the work is done (not just what)?
- Is the individual economically dependent on Call360?
- Is the work integral to Call360's business?

If the answer to these questions is "yes," a permanent employment contract may be required. HR and Legal must be consulted before any long-term contractor arrangement.

## Contractor Conduct and Access

Contractors are expected to:
- Adhere to Call360's Code of Conduct and security policies
- Use only the systems and data necessary for their scope of work
- Not disclose Call360 confidential information

## Benefits and Leave

Contractors are self-employed and not entitled to Call360's employee benefits (vacation, sick leave, pension, L&D budget, etc.).

## Termination of Engagement

Engagements end on the agreed date or when the work is completed. Either party may terminate early per the terms of the Service Agreement.
