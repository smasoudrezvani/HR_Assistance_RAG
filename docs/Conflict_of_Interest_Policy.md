# Conflict of Interest Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

A conflict of interest arises when a personal interest or outside relationship could — or could appear to — influence an employee's judgment or decisions on behalf of Call360. This policy helps employees identify and manage such situations.

## Types of Conflicts

### Financial Interests
- Owning a significant financial stake in a competitor, supplier, or business partner
- Receiving personal financial benefits (beyond normal salary and bonus) from a third party in connection with Call360 work

### Outside Employment or Business
- Working for a competitor in a capacity that conflicts with your Call360 role
- Operating a personal business that competes with Call360 or uses Call360's resources
- Consulting for a third party on matters directly related to your Call360 role without approval

### Relationships
- Supervising or making employment decisions about a close family member or romantic partner
- Awarding contracts, promotions, or favorable decisions to personal connections

### Gifts and Hospitality
See the Gift and Entertainment Policy for specific guidance on receiving or giving gifts.

## Disclosure Requirement

Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest promptly to their manager and hr@call360.com. Disclosures are reviewed by HR and Legal.

## How to Disclose

Submit a Conflict of Interest Disclosure Form via PeopleBase → HR Forms → Conflict of Interest Disclosure. Include:
- Nature of the potential conflict
- Parties involved
- How you believe it may (or may not) affect your work at Call360

## Resolution

Following disclosure, HR and the employee's manager will determine the appropriate resolution. Options include:
- No action required (conflict is minor or non-material)
- Recusal from specific decisions or projects
- Divestment of a financial interest
- Changing reporting lines or responsibilities

## Annual Declaration

All employees at L3 and above are required to complete an annual Conflict of Interest Declaration in PeopleBase each January.

## Consequences of Non-Disclosure

Failure to disclose a known conflict of interest is a breach of the Code of Conduct and may result in disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal.

## Questions

Contact hr@call360.com or legal@call360.com
