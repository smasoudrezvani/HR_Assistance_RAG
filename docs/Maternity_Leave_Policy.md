# Maternity Leave Policy
**Call360 | Version 2.1 | Effective January 2026**

## Purpose

Call360 fully supports expectant and new mothers through maternity leave. This policy outlines entitlements, notification requirements, and return-to-work support.

## Entitlements by Region

### Netherlands (NL)
- **16 weeks paid maternity leave** at 100% of salary
- At least **4 weeks must be taken before** the expected birth date
- The remaining weeks are taken after birth
- Leave is funded through the WAZO benefit; Call360 applies on your behalf
- Annual leave continues to accrue during maternity leave

### South Africa (ZA)
- **4 consecutive months unpaid maternity leave** (as per the Basic Conditions of Employment Act)
- Leave may begin no earlier than 4 weeks before the expected birth
- Employees may not return to work within 6 weeks of birth without a medical certificate confirming fitness to do so
- Employees may apply for UIF (Unemployment Insurance Fund) maternity benefits during this period

### Remote (Global)
- **8 consecutive weeks paid maternity leave** at 100% salary
- Leave can be taken from any point within 4 weeks before the expected birth
- Annual leave continues to accrue during this period

## Surrogacy

In the Netherlands: birth mother via surrogacy receives standard maternity leave entitlement.
In South Africa: employees who have entered into a surrogacy agreement are entitled to 10 consecutive weeks of unpaid leave from the birth.

## Notification Requirements

Employees must inform the People & Culture team:
- **At least 3 months before** the intended start of maternity leave (NL & Remote)
- **At least 4 weeks before** the intended start (ZA)

Notification should include:
- Expected birth date (confirmed by GP or midwife letter)
- Intended start date of maternity leave
- Expected return-to-work date

Notify HR by emailing hr@call360.com and updating PeopleBase under **Time Off → Maternity Leave**.

## Return to Work

Before returning, employees should:
- Confirm their return date with HR at least 4 weeks in advance
- Discuss any workplace adjustments needed (e.g., phased return, breastfeeding arrangements)
- Schedule a check-in meeting with their manager within the first week back

## Support Resources

- Access to the Employee Assistance Program (EAP) for mental health and parenting support
- Call360 managers complete a return-to-work onboarding with returning parents
