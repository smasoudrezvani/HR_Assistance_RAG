# Workplace Health and Safety Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.2 | Effective January 2026**

## Policy Statement

Call360 is committed to providing a safe and healthy working environment for all employees, contractors, and visitors. We comply with all applicable health and safety laws and take a proactive approach to identifying and managing workplace risks.

## Scope

This policy applies to all Call360 workplaces including the Amsterdam office, Cape Town office, and remote employee home workspaces.

## Responsibilities

### Employer (Call360)
- Maintain safe premises, equipment, and work systems
- Conduct regular risk assessments
- Provide relevant health and safety training
- Investigate all workplace incidents and near-misses
- Provide Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) where required

### Employees
- Take reasonable care of their own health and safety
- Report hazards, near-misses, and incidents immediately
- Follow all health and safety instructions and procedures
- Not misuse or interfere with safety equipment
- Participate in safety training when required

### Managers
- Ensure their team is aware of and follows safety procedures
- Conduct team-level risk assessments when relevant
- Respond promptly to reported hazards

## Risk Assessment

A general workplace risk assessment is conducted:
- At each office, at least once per year
- After any significant incident or near-miss
- When work practices or the workplace changes significantly

Remote employees are responsible for ensuring their home workspace meets basic ergonomic and safety standards (see Ergonomics Policy).

## Incident Reporting

All workplace incidents, accidents, and near-misses must be reported within 24 hours:
- **Email**: safety@call360.com
- **PeopleBase**: Safety → Report Incident
- Serious injuries: contact emergency services immediately, then notify the office manager and HR

HR will document and investigate all incidents. Reports are kept confidential where possible.

## First Aid

- First aid kits are located at all office reception areas and in the kitchen
- Trained first aiders are designated per office floor — list available in Confluence → Facilities
- In an emergency: call 112 (NL) or 10177 (ZA) immediately

## Mental Health and Wellbeing

Psychological safety is as important as physical safety. See the Mental Health Policy and Employee Assistance Program for support resources.

## Training

Health and safety awareness training is mandatory for:
- All new employees (completed within first 30 days)
- All employees after a significant workplace incident
