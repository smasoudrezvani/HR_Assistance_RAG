# Vacation and Annual Leave Policy
**Call360 | Version 2.0 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

Call360 offers competitive vacation entitlements to support rest, personal time, and wellbeing. This policy outlines entitlements by region, how to request time off, and how leave accrues.

## Vacation Entitlements by Region

### Netherlands (NL) Employees
- **25 days per calendar year** (pro-rated for part-year employment)
- 20 statutory days expire by 30 June of the following year
- The remaining 5 days can be carried over for up to 5 years
- Public holidays are in addition to vacation days

### South Africa (ZA) Employees
- **15 days per calendar year** (pro-rated)
- Unused leave up to 30 days may be carried forward; excess is forfeited at end of June
- Public holidays are in addition to vacation days

### Remote (Global) Employees
- **20 days per calendar year** (pro-rated)
- Up to 10 days can be carried over to the following year, expiring after 6 months
- Public holidays in the employee's home country are drawn from the 20-day balance

## Accrual

Leave accrues monthly from the employee's start date. New employees can access accrued leave during their probation period but should plan in advance.

## How to Request Leave

1. Log in to PeopleBase
2. Navigate to **Time Off → Request Leave**
3. Select the relevant leave type and dates
4. Submit for manager approval

**Notice periods recommended:**
- 1–3 days: at least 1 week in advance
- 1–2 weeks: at least 2 weeks in advance
- 2 weeks or longer: at least 4 weeks in advance

Requests are not automatically approved. Manager approval is required, taking into account team capacity and business needs.

## Public Holidays

Public holidays are observed per the laws of the employee's country of employment. A schedule of public holidays is published in PeopleBase at the start of each year.

Employees may be eligible to swap public holidays — see the Public Holiday Swap Policy.

## Leave at Year-End

Call360 typically observes a company-wide break over the December–January holiday period (dates announced annually). This period uses employees' vacation days unless otherwise stated.

## Leave During Notice Period

Leave may not be accrued or taken during a contractual notice period unless otherwise agreed with HR and your manager.

## Unused Leave on Termination

Accrued but unused vacation days are paid out upon termination of employment, subject to local law.
