# Remote Work Policy
**Call360 | Version 1.4 | Effective January 2026**

## Overview

Call360 is a distributed-first company with employees working from our Amsterdam and Cape Town offices as well as remotely across multiple countries. This policy outlines expectations, eligibility, and guidelines for remote work arrangements.

## Eligibility

Remote work is available to employees whose roles allow it, at the discretion of their manager and the People & Culture team. Eligibility is determined based on:

- Role requirements and collaboration needs
- Performance track record
- Reliable access to a suitable work environment
- Time zone compatibility with the team

Some roles — such as on-site customer support or office operations — are not eligible for remote work.

## Types of Remote Work

### Fully Remote
Employees contracted as remote work from their home country. These employees are expected to:
- Have a designated, professional workspace
- Maintain reliable, high-speed internet (minimum 25 Mbps)
- Be reachable via Slack and email during agreed working hours

### Hybrid
Amsterdam and Cape Town office employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to team arrangements and manager approval.

### Temporary Remote / Working Abroad
Employees may work temporarily from another location for up to 2 weeks per year, subject to:
- Prior approval from their manager
- Confirmation of reliable internet connectivity
- At least 4 hours of overlap with their core team's working hours
- Travel insurance in place for the full period (employee responsibility)
- Compliance with tax obligations in the host country (HR can advise)

## Working Hours and Availability

- Core collaboration hours are **09:00–14:00 CET** for Amsterdam-aligned teams
- Remote employees must agree working hours with their manager at the start of employment
- All employees must attend scheduled meetings they have accepted, regardless of location
- Status in Slack should reflect availability (available, in a meeting, away)

## Equipment

- Call360 provides a laptop and standard peripherals to all employees
- Remote employees receive a one-time home office setup allowance (see: Work From Home Equipment Allowance policy)
- Employees are responsible for the security and safe return of all company equipment

## Security Obligations

When working remotely, employees must:
- Use the Call360 VPN when accessing internal systems
- Lock their laptop when away from their desk
- Not work from public locations where screens may be visible without a privacy screen
- Never leave company equipment unattended in public places

## Communication Expectations

Remote employees are expected to:
- Respond to Slack messages within 2 hours during working hours
- Reply to emails within 1 business day
- Proactively communicate blockers, availability changes, or delays to their manager
- Keep their calendar up to date

## Approval Process

Request remote or hybrid arrangements by discussing with your manager and completing the Remote Work Agreement form in PeopleBase.

Contact hr@call360.com with questions.
